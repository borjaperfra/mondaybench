PLATFORM CUTOVER - WORKING FILE

This file collects the material that has accumulated around the platform work.
All times are on a 24-hour clock. Everything described happens on the same day.

===============================================================================
1. WHERE THIS CAME FROM
===============================================================================

The platform group has been carrying two parallel programmes since the spring.
The first is the reporting warehouse consolidation, which finished ahead of
schedule and is no longer discussed here. The second is the one this file is
about. It was originally scoped as a three-day exercise and then compressed into
a single day after the vendor confirmed that the intermediate state could be
skipped.

Teodor wrote the first draft of the plan in April. Ines revised it twice, once
after the capacity review and once after the vendor call. Marek maintains the
runbook and has asked that any change to it go through him rather than being
edited in place, because the June incident was traced to two people editing the
same runbook section within the same hour.

None of that history changes the plan. It is recorded because people keep
asking why the plan looks the way it does.

===============================================================================
2. RUNBOOK EXTRACT - TIMINGS, PART ONE
===============================================================================

The timings below were measured during the two rehearsals and are the numbers
the plan uses.

The export snapshot takes 60 minutes.

The schema upgrade takes 90 minutes.

Marek notes that the rehearsal figures were taken on the rehearsal hardware,
which is the same generation as production. The reporting warehouse work used
a different generation, so its numbers are not comparable and should not be
used here.

The index rebuild takes 120 minutes.

The permissions sync takes 45 minutes.

===============================================================================
3. CAPACITY AND COST NOTES (BACKGROUND ONLY)
===============================================================================

The capacity review in March found that the platform runs at about 41% of
provisioned headroom on a normal weekday, rising to 68% during the month-end
close. Neither figure constrains the plan, because the work described here is
not scheduled during a close.

Sofia's cost note puts the incremental infrastructure cost of the day at
roughly 2,400 units, most of it in temporary storage for the intermediate
state. Finance has approved it. The approval expires at the end of the quarter,
which is not a constraint on the day itself.

The event bus carries about 18,000 messages a minute at peak. It is untouched by
this work. The billing reconciler is also untouched, although Ruth has asked to
be told when the day is over so that she can restart her nightly comparison job.

5. RUNBOOK EXTRACT - ORDERING
===============================================================================

The ordering below is the part of the runbook Marek considers settled.

The schema upgrade cannot start until the export snapshot has finished.

The index rebuild cannot start until the schema upgrade has finished.

The smoke checks cannot start until both the index rebuild and the permissions sync have finished.

The consumer replay cannot start until the smoke checks have finished.

The final verification cannot start until the consumer replay has finished.

Marek adds that the runbook used to contain a sixth ordering rule about the
intermediate state. It was removed when the vendor confirmed the intermediate
state could be skipped, and it is not reinstated anywhere.

8. SHARED EQUIPMENT
===============================================================================

There is one piece of shared equipment in play.

The index rebuild and the permissions sync each need exclusive use of the staging cluster, so they cannot overlap.

An optional cache warm of 50 minutes can be run on the staging cluster, but it is not required for the cutover.

Marek's view, recorded for completeness, is that the optional item is a nice
thing to have when there is room for it and a distraction when there is not.

===============================================================================
9. GROUND RULES
===============================================================================

Work on the cutover may begin no earlier than 07:00.

One person performs one task at a time, no task may be paused once it has started, and a task must fit entirely inside the availability window of the person performing it.

The whole cutover must be complete by 19:00.

The permissions sync runs before the schema upgrade.
