1. **Customer Segment Decision (Reduce investment in one segment?): DO NOT reduce investment broadly across Mid-Market or SMB without separating sub-tiers.**
   - **Mid-Market** suffered the month's largest single ARR loss (€180,000 from Nakatomi) and severe contraction risk at Soylent, driven by unresolved API errors and performance bottlenecks—cutting investment here will trigger further enterprise-scale churn.

Paid Search claimed €238,800 in October attributed revenue, but **€174,000 (72.9%) was from existing Enterprise customers** (Stark Industries, Umbrella Health, Globex) who have been with Northstar for years, likely clicking branded search links before expanding. 
   - Top-of-funnel Paid Search lead-to-paid-trial conversion dropped from **8.46% to 6.44%**, and net-new logo ARR from Paid Search was only €64,800 across 900 leads.
3. **Engineering & Product Focus (Which plan deserves focus?): Focus on the PRO PLAN architecture, while protecting Enterprise stability.**
   - **The Pro plan is Northstar’s economic engine** (62.7% gross margin vs. Enterprise’s 29.5%), but it has hit an operational ceiling: Pro customers generated **100% of all rate-limiting tickets** (spiking 160% MoM from 5 to 13 tickets). 4. **CFO Request (CAC Payback by Channel): CANNOT BE CALCULATED.**
   - The data pack contains **zero cost or spend data** for marketing channels, campaigns, or sales headcount. Calculating CAC or payback periods without spend data is methodologically impossible; any presented CAC figure would be pure speculation.

## 2. Revenue & Customer Health

### A. Headline Revenue vs. Underlying MRR Reality
Naively summing `revenue.csv` suggests cash invoicing jumped from **€797,100** in September to **€887,400** in October (+€90,300). This headline surge is distorted by three non-operational artifacts:
1. **Billing Frequency Shift (Cash Spike):** Gringotts converted from monthly billing (€10,000/mo) to an annual contract (`INV-2026-10-0236`), generating a one-time cash collection of **€120,000** in October (a +€110,000 cash bump over September), while recurring normalized revenue remained flat at €10,000/mo.
2. **Data Pipeline Duplicate:** Transaction `INV-2026-10-0209` for Soylent was ingested twice (once via `stripe` on Oct 1 and once via `billing_sync` on Oct 2), artificially inflating invoicing by **€19,500**.
3. **Professional Services Collapse:** PS revenue dropped from **€51,000** in September (Globex €33k, Hooli €18k) to just **€5,700** in October (Aperture Labs), down 88.8%.

```
Normalized Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Progression:
- September MRR (plans.csv): €659,000 (Ent) + €63,000 (Pro) + €24,100 (Starter) = €746,100
- October Invoiced MRR:     €659,500 (Ent) + €66,200 (Pro) + €32,000 (Starter) = €757,700 (+1.55%)

- Effective Nov 1 Run-Rate: €659,500 (Ent) + €51,200 (Pro) + €21,700 (Starter) = €732,400 (-1.84% vs Sep)
```

Because October invoices were issued between October 1st and 3rd, **all 5 churned customers paid for October in full**. Consequently, the true revenue impact has not yet appeared in October's billed MRR, but it creates an immediate **€25,300/month headwind (-€303,600 ARR)** entering November.

## 5. Strategic Recommendations

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                   DECISION ROADMAP SUMMARY                                        |
+------------------------------+--------------------+-----------------------------------------------+
| Decision Issue               | Verdict            | Strategic Action                              |
+------------------------------+--------------------+-----------------------------------------------+
| 1. Customer Segment Spend    | REJECT BROAD CUTS  | Protect Mid-Market/SMB Pro; automate Starter. |
| 2. Paid Search Spend         | DO NOT INCREASE    | Reallocate to Content/Partner; fix attribution.|
| 3. Engineering Focus         | PRO PLAN ARCH      | Overhaul rate-limits & auto-upgrade pipeline. |
+------------------------------+--------------------+-----------------------------------------------+
```

### Decision 1: Customer Segment Investment
- **Do not reduce investment in Mid-Market.** Mid-Market represents €1.71M in ARR and serves as Northstar's expansion pipeline into Enterprise (e.g., Hooli expanded +€36k ARR). Nakatomi’s churn (€180k ARR) and Soylent's contraction (-€30k ARR) were caused by technical debt (API reliability and performance), not customer segment economics. Reducing investment here will accelerate churn among accounts that pay €100k–€250k ARR.
- **Bifurcate SMB:**
  - **SMB Pro (Retain & Nurture):** Customers like Wonka, Oscorp, and Planet Express generate high gross margins and have massive API volume.
  - **SMB Starter (Restructure):** Starter customer unit economics will deteriorate to 0% gross margin in November post-churn. Transition Starter to a strict **no-touch self-serve model**: eliminate dedicated CS support, direct all Starter tickets to community/automated documentation, and require annual upfront commitments to eliminate month-to-month churn.

### Decision 2: Acquisition Channel Spend
- **Reject Growth’s request to increase Paid Search spend.**
  - Paid Search lead-to-trial conversion is degrading (down 200 bps to 6.44%).
  - The apparent surge in revenue is an attribution artifact of existing enterprise customers clicking search ads prior to pre-negotiated expansions.
- **Channel Strategy Reallocation:**
  - Shift incremental growth budget into **Partner** and **Content**. Partner delivers a 22.7% trial rate and proven mid-market deal size (Vandelay, €132k). Content yields double the trial conversion of Paid Search (10.3%) with zero brand cannibalism.
  - Implement negative keyword lists on Paid Search to stop bidding on existing customer brand and login queries.

### Decision 3: Product & Engineering Roadmap
- **Designate the Pro Plan as the Q4 Engineering Focus:**
  1. **Dynamic Rate-Limiting & Burst Handling:** Replace hard request blocking with burst queuing and soft limits. Pro accounts currently hitting walls (Wonka, Aperture, Vandelay) represent immediate expansion opportunities.
  2. **Automated Usage Metering & Overage Billing:** Implement automated billing for requests exceeding the 600,000 tier limit, eliminating manual renewal reconciliation and capturing unrealized revenue from Wonka’s 1.02M requests.
  3. **In-App Enterprise Upgrade Pathway:** Build seamless self-serve migration from Pro to Enterprise plans when usage consistently exceeds 600k requests/month.
- **Secondary Operations Task Force (Enterprise Retention):**
  - Form an immediate engineering SWAT team to resolve Wayne Enterprises’ pending high-severity performance ticket (`TCK-10-0071`) and stabilize Soylent’s API errors (`TCK-10-0087`) to protect **€1.242M in ARR** over the next 30 days.

## 6. Uncertainties & Data Integrity Issues

Before finalize Q4 forecasts, the following technical and operational data issues must be resolved:

1. **Duplicate Invoice Ingestion (`revenue.csv`):**
   - Transaction `INV-2026-10-0209` for Soylent (€19,500) appears twice with identical transaction IDs from two different sources (`stripe` on 2026-10-01 and `billing_sync` on 2026-10-02). Data engineering must implement deduplication on `transaction_id`.
2. **Absence of Marketing Cost Data:**
   - `funnel.csv` and `acquisition.csv` provide lead counts and conversions but **no channel spend, cost-per-lead, or sales overhead**. The CFO’s request for CAC payback cannot be answered until marketing spend is integrated into the review pack.
3. **Flawed Expansion Attribution Model:**
   - The marketing platform awards 100% last-touch credit to acquisition channels for contract expansions executed by multi-year customers (e.g., Stark Industries, customer since June 2023, attributed €84k to a Paid Search campaign started in Sept 2026). Marketing must decouple **New Logo Attribution** from **Account Management / Expansion Reporting**.
4. **Disconnection Between Funnel Trials and Paying Customers:**
   - `funnel.csv` measures "conversions" as *paid trials initiated within the month*. It does not track *trial-to-paid subscription conversion*. As a result, funnel conversions cannot be reconciled directly against customer additions in `customers.csv`.
5. **Untracked Usage Overages:**
   - `plans.csv` states that usage exceeding plan limits is "billed separately at renewal." Multiple customers (Wonka, Aperture, Vandelay) operated significantly above quota in September and October. Because overages are unbilled until renewal, current recurring revenue figures underestimate the true infrastructure cost burden created by over-quota accounts.
