## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Headline metrics for October suggest modest growth, with total Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) rising from **€746,100 to €757,700 (+1.55%)** and cash collections reaching **€873,400** (bolstered by Gringotts’ €120,000 annual prepayment). However, underlying data reveals acute structural risks and answers the three pending decisions:

1. **Reduce investment in one customer segment? YES, specifically the low-end SMB Starter tier, NOT Mid-market or all SMBs.**  
   Four SMB Starter accounts churned in October (**30.8% of the Starter customer base; €123,600 ARR lost**). Post-churn Starter gross margin in November will fall toward **0–10%** due to disproportionate support overhead. However, SMB Pro accounts are expanding and must be preserved.
2. **Increase spend on one acquisition channel? NO, specifically against Paid Search.**  
   Paid Search's apparent revenue explosion (from €36,000 in September to €238,800 in October) is **72.9% artificial**—the result of existing enterprise accounts (Stark Industries, Umbrella Health, Globex) clicking branded search ads around contract milestones. Real top-of-funnel lead-to-trial conversion dropped from **8.46% to 6.44%**, and acquisition spend data is entirely absent, making CAC payback uncalculable.
3. **Product plan deserving engineering focus? The ENTERPRISE PLAN (Primary) and PRO PLAN (Secondary).**  
   Enterprise drives **87.0% of MRR**, but its gross margin eroded to **29.52%** in October, plagued by platform stability issues (e.g., Soylent’s critical API failure led to a 27% usage drop). Engineering must prioritize Enterprise inference cost optimization and reliability. Simultaneously, the Pro plan’s rigid 600,000 request cap is causing extreme ticket friction (68% of October Pro tickets were `rate_limits`), throttling high-growth accounts like Wonka Industries.
4. **CFO Request (CAC Payback): CANNOT BE CALCULATED.**  
   Northstar does not track or supply acquisition cost/spend data anywhere in the pack.

### The Attribution Trap in Paid Search
Growth points to €238,800 in attributed revenue in October. Deconstructing `acquisition.csv` reveals:
1. **Existing Customer Expansion Cannibalization:** **€174,000 (72.9%)** of October's Paid Search revenue came from existing enterprise accounts:
   * **Stark Industries:** €84,000 expansion (Customer since June 2023)
   * **Umbrella Health:** €48,000 expansion (Customer since January 2024)
   * **Globex:** €42,000 expansion (Customer since March 2024)
   * *In September, Hooli's €36,000 expansion was similarly misattributed to Paid Search.*
2. **The Mechanism:** The marketing campaign started on `2026-09-28`. Longstanding enterprise accounts navigating to Northstar via search ads during renewal/upsell windows were credited to Paid Search under a last-touch attribution model.
3. **True New-Logo Generation:** Paid Search delivered only two new customers in October: Spacely-sprockets (€38,400 ARR) and Gekko-co (€26,400 ARR), totaling **€64,800 ARR**, and **zero** new logos in September.
4. **Funnel Deterioration:** Paid Search lead volume surged +73.1% (520 to 900), but conversions to paid trial rose only +31.8% (44 to 58). The lead-to-trial conversion rate dropped from **8.46% to 6.44%**. Marginal conversion on the 380 incremental leads was just **3.68%** (14/380).

Any CAC payback figure presented to leadership would be fictitious.

### Unit Economic Insights

#### 1. Pro Plan: The Strongest Unit Economics, Crippled by Rate Limits
* **Gross Margin:** Operates at **62.7%–67.5%**, double the margin profile of Enterprise and Starter.
* **Cost Efficiency:** Direct COGS per 1,000 requests in October was just **€6.21** (vs. €17.58 for Enterprise and €19.78 for Starter).
* **The Structural Bottleneck:** Pro includes 600,000 requests/month. Power users are severely penalized. In October, **13 of the 19 support tickets raised by Pro customers (68.4%) were for `rate_limits`**:
  * Wonka Industries: 1,020,000 requests (4 rate limit tickets)
  * Aperture Labs: 705,000 requests (2 rate limit tickets)
  * Vandelay: 690,000 requests (1 rate limit ticket)
  * Prestige Worldwide: 545,000 requests (2 rate limit tickets)
  * Oscorp Labs, Pied Piper, Planet Express: all logged rate limit tickets.
* Because usage beyond the included allotment is billed only at annual renewal, Northstar incurs unbilled infrastructure strain while frustrating high-growth clients.

#### 2. Enterprise Plan: High Revenue Contribution, Alarmingly Low Margins
* **Revenue Share:** Generates **87.0%** of company MRR (€659,500).
* **Margin Compression:** Gross margin fell to **29.52%** in October. Total direct costs reached **€464,800/month** (€296,800 infrastructure + €168,000 dedicated CS/support).
* **Cost Driver:** Enterprise accounts average 1.89M requests/month, but high-concurrency interactive workloads drive up GPU/inference provisioning costs. Acme proved that shifting to scheduled batch processing slashes interactive volume by 45% without damaging token throughput.

#### 3. Starter Plan: The Weakest Link
* **False Growth:** MRR increased from €24,100 to €32,000 on October 1st as 4 new accounts joined.
* **Churn Wipeout:** 4 accounts churned in October (Slate-rock, Mega-lo-mart, Dinoco, Buy-n-large), representing **€10,300/mo in lost revenue**.
* **November Reality:** Starter MRR will drop to **€21,700**. If October direct costs (€21,700) remain static, **Starter gross margin will hit 0.0%**.
* **Support Drag:** Support cost per Starter account in October was **€792/month** on an average revenue of €2,461/month—a 32.2% support cost burden. Starter costs **€19.78 per 1,000 requests**, the highest across all plans.

## 5. RECOMMENDATIONS

### Decision 1: Customer Segment Investment (Finance Query)
* **Action: Divest from high-touch Starter SMB; Protect SMB Pro and Mid-market.**
  1. **Automate Starter:** Remove dedicated CS/support from the Starter plan. Transition Starter entirely to self-serve documentation and community support to reduce the €10,300/mo support cost line.
  2. **Do Not Defund SMB Broadly:** Defunding all SMBs would harm high-performing accounts like Wonka Industries (€50.4k ARR, 1.02M requests) and Planet Express (+49.3% usage growth in October). Segment strategy by plan tier (Starter vs. Pro), not by raw company size.
  3. **Intervene in Mid-Market (Soylent):** Assign an executive sponsor and principal engineer immediately to stabilize Soylent before their contract is canceled.

### Decision 2: Acquisition Channel Spend (Growth Query)
* **Action: Freeze Paid Search spend increases; Overhaul marketing attribution.**
  1. **Deny Budget Request:** Do not increase Paid Search spend. The marginal trial conversion rate has deteriorated to 3.68%.
  2. **Fix Attribution Modeling:** Implement multi-touch or first-touch attribution that excludes existing customers from performance marketing campaigns. Stop crediting enterprise contract expansions to Google Search ads.
  3. **Invest in Partner & Content:** Partner delivered the highest lead-to-trial rate (25.0% in Sep, 22.7% in Oct) and landed Vandelay (€132,000 ARR). Content generated stable, high-quality conversions (10.3%–11.9%) and landed €63,600 ARR in October.

### Decision 3: Engineering Focus Next Quarter (Product Query)
* **Action: Primary focus on ENTERPRISE PLATFORM EFFICIENCY & RELIABILITY; Secondary focus on PRO TIER USAGE-BASED BILLING.**
  1. **Enterprise Reliability & Batch Infrastructure (Primary):**
     * Build native scheduled batch inference APIs (modeled on Acme’s reporting implementation) to shift workloads away from peak interactive capacity, directly targeting Enterprise’s 70.5% COGS.
     * Resolve recurring `api_errors` and `performance` degradation affecting enterprise infrastructure (Stark, Wayne, Contoso, Soylent).
  2. **Pro Plan Monetization & Rate Limit Overhaul (Secondary):**
     * Replace rigid rate limit cutoffs with dynamic concurrency limits and automated overage billing.
     * Create an automated "Bridge to Enterprise" upgrade pathway for accounts exceeding 600,000 requests (Wonka, Aperture, Vandelay) to capture immediate expansion revenue.

## 6. UNCERTAINTIES & DATA ISSUES

1. **Complete Absence of Marketing Cost Data:**  
   The dataset does not contain acquisition spend, CPCs, campaign budgets, or sales compensation. Consequently, **CAC, Blended CAC, and CAC Payback Periods cannot be calculated**. Growth and Finance must integrate ad platform spend before capital allocation reviews.
2. **Flawed Attribution Logic:**  
   Expansion bookings from enterprise customers with multi-year tenure (Stark, Umbrella, Globex, Hooli, Cyberdyne, Massive Dynamic) are attributed to short-term marketing campaigns. This creates a misleading appearance of marketing ROI.
3. **Duplicate Invoice Record:**  
   Invoice `INV-2026-10-0209` for customer `soylent` (€19,500) appears twice in `revenue.csv`: once on 2026-10-01 from `stripe` and once on 2026-10-02 from `billing_sync`. It must be confirmed whether Soylent was double-charged or if this is a data pipeline sync error.
4. **Discrepancy Between Invoicing and Revenue Recognition:**  
   Gringotts was invoiced €120,000 under `annual_subscription` in October (`INV-2026-10-0236`). In `plans.csv`, this is recognized as €10,000/month. Conversely, churned customers who paid on October 1st are recognized as full active customers throughout October in `plans.csv`. November financial models must be adjusted downward by €25,300 MRR.
5. **Overage Revenue Latency:**  
   Per `plans.csv` documentation, usage beyond included plan requests is billed "separately at renewal." Substantial unbilled overage consumption is currently accumulating without real-time revenue recognition (e.g., Wonka consumed 1,020,000 requests against a 600,000 limit).
6. **Usage Pipeline ID Discrepancies:**  
   `customers.csv` lists `wayne-enterprises` with `usage_account_id` = `wayne_ent`, and `prestige-worldwide` with `prestige_ww`. While mapped successfully in this analysis, manual reconciliation is required due to the lack of enforced relational keys across billing and telemetry logs.
