### What Happened
- **11:42 UTC:** Incident began when automated alerts fired for elevated 502 errors on `POST /v1/batch/jobs`.
- **Scope:** The issue was strictly isolated to the Batch API. Northstar’s other surfaces (Inference, Embeddings, and Files APIs) remained completely healthy. Upstream object-storage providers were confirmed fully operational.
- **12:58 UTC:** Mitigation applied by shifting all Batch API traffic to our secondary worker fleet (fleet-b) and draining the degraded fleet (fleet-a) out of rotation. Error rates dropped from peak failure rates to 0.4% within two minutes.
- **13:52 UTC:** Failed job re-drives reached ~60% completion with zero re-drive errors.

### Who Is Hit & Overall Impact (11:42 – 12:58 UTC)
- **Headline Impact:** Failed Batch API requests accounted for **29.9%** of all Batch API traffic during the incident window (17,602 failures out of 58,900 total Batch requests).
- **Platform Context:** Across all Northstar APIs, 842,000 total requests were processed during the window; batch failures represented **2.1%** of total platform volume.
- **Customer Breakdown:** Northstar has 36 total customer accounts; 9 accounts use the Batch API.
  - **7 accounts experienced failures** (all pinned to fleet-a):
    - *stark-industries:* 5,180 failures (out of 14,200 requests)
    - *contoso:* 3,410 failures (out of 9,400 requests)
    - *wayne-enterprises:* 2,790 failures (out of 7,850 requests)
    - *globex:* 2,240 failures (out of 6,100 requests)
    - *wonka-industries:* 2,020 failures (out of 5,900 requests)
    - *umbrella-health:* 1,560 failures (out of 4,300 requests)
    - *initech:* 402 failures (out of 1,150 requests)
  - **2 Batch API accounts experienced zero failures:** *acme-corp* (6,700 requests) and *aperture-labs* (3,300 requests).
  - **27 accounts** do not use the Batch API and had zero exposure.

### Commercial & SLA Exposure
- **Contoso** is the only impacted customer with an SLA providing service credits (Schedule B). They have submitted a support ticket asking if this triggers a credit against their 99.9% Monthly Availability commitment.
- **Contractual Reality:** Under Schedule B, Monthly Availability is calculated across all Covered Services (Batch, Inference, and Files) across the entire calendar month after the month ends. This incident alone does not automatically determine a breach; determination occurs after November concludes.
- The other 6 impacted accounts are on standard terms with no service-credit provisions.
