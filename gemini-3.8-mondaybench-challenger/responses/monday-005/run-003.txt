## CEO BRIEFING

### Executive Summary
- **Current Status:** Incident INC-2026-1109-01 is **OPEN but MITIGATED**. 
- **Operational State:** All Batch API traffic was successfully shifted to our healthy worker fleet (fleet-b) at 12:58 UTC. Error rates on `POST /v1/batch/jobs` dropped immediately from peak failure levels to 0.4% and are currently holding steady at 0.3% (baseline is 0.2%).
- **Verification Underway:** A mandatory 90-minute stability verification window opened at 13:00 UTC and will close at 14:30 UTC (10 minutes remaining). Per Northstar incident policy, the incident cannot and will not be declared resolved until this window completes clean.
- **Data Recovery:** An automated re-drive of failed submissions is underway. As of 13:52 UTC, approximately 60% of failed jobs have been re-driven with zero failures.

### Incident Timeline & Root Cause Status
- **11:31 UTC:** Routine deployment of `batch-api@2026.11.09-r3` completed across both worker fleets (fleet-a and fleet-b). Changes included three dependency updates and lowering the per-worker database connection pool limit from 80 to 24.
- **11:38 UTC:** Batch submission volume on fleet-a surged to ~3x the morning average, driven by a large scheduled job from Wonka Industries.
- **11:42 UTC (Incident Start):** Alert fired for elevated 502 errors on `POST /v1/batch/jobs` isolated to fleet-a. Fleet-b remained healthy throughout.
- **11:47 UTC:** Upstream object storage was verified green (and the Files API, which uses the same provider, stayed healthy throughout), ruling out upstream provider failure.
- **12:58 UTC (Mitigation Applied):** All Batch API traffic was cut over to fleet-b. Degraded fleet-a was drained and isolated to preserve state for investigation.
- **Root Cause:** **Not yet established.** Two untested hypotheses remain active:
  1. *Database connection pool reduction* in release `r3` (workers on fleet-a were visibly queueing for database connections; however, `r3` was also deployed to fleet-b with no degradation).
  2. *Concentrated load spike* on fleet-a (though fleet-a handled a larger peak on 2026-11-03 without errors).
  - The likely cause is an interaction between the lower pool limit and the volume spike. A rollback test was deliberately deferred to avoid wiping forensic state on fleet-a.

### Customer Impact & Exposure
- **Service Scope:** Isolated strictly to the **Batch API**. Real-time inference services, Embeddings API, and Files API experienced 100% normal operations.
- **Accounts Affected:** **7 accounts** experienced failures (out of 9 accounts that use the Batch API, and 36 total Northstar customer accounts). All 7 affected accounts were pinned to fleet-a. The 2 accounts on fleet-b had zero failures.
- **Traffic Volume (11:42–12:58 UTC window):**
  - **Batch API Traffic:** 17,602 failed requests out of 58,900 total Batch submissions (**29.9% failure rate** across the window).
  - **Platform-Wide Traffic:** 17,602 failures out of 842,000 total platform requests across all API surfaces (**2.1% platform failure rate**).
- **Commercial & SLA Exposure:**
  - **Contoso** is the **only** affected customer with a contract containing service-level credits (Schedule B). Contoso has already submitted two support tickets inquiring about SLA credits.
  - *SLA Reality:* Schedule B commits to 99.9% monthly availability across all Core APIs (Inference, Batch, Files) combined, calculated after the close of the calendar month. A 76-minute partial degradation on the Batch API alone will be heavily diluted across Contoso’s full-month multi-API traffic. We cannot confirm mid-month whether a breach will occur.
  - The other 6 affected accounts (Stark Industries, Wayne Enterprises, Globex, Wonka Industries, Umbrella Health, Initech) are on standard terms with no service-credit provisions.

### Internal Security Discovery (Non-Contributing)
- During triage configuration reviews, engineering noted that the batch worker database role holds read/write permissions to the billing schema. This standing over-permission played **no role** in the incident. Ticket `SEC-411` has been logged for Marcus Oyelaran to restrict permissions to read-only on the two required tables this week.
