## CEO BRIEFING

**State of play at 14:20 UTC — incident INC-2026-1109-01 (Batch API).**

At 11:42 UTC the Batch API began returning elevated 502 errors on job submissions. The errors were confined to one of the two internal worker fleets (fleet-a). A release earlier that morning (r3, 11:31 UTC) included a database connection-pool change and is the leading hypothesis, but it is not confirmed. fleet-a was also running about 3x its normal volume from a large scheduled job. The upstream storage provider was ruled out. Root cause is not yet established.

At 12:58 UTC all Batch API traffic was moved to the healthy fleet (fleet-b). The error rate fell to 0.4% within two minutes (baseline 0.2%) and is holding at 0.3%. A 90-minute verification window is open until 14:30 UTC. The incident is mitigated but not resolved.

**Impact:** Seven accounts were affected, all on fleet-a. In the incident window (11:42–12:58), the Batch API handled 58,900 requests, of which 17,602 failed — 29.9% of Batch API requests in the window, and about 7% of platform-wide traffic (842,000 requests) in the same window. Affected accounts: stark-industries, contoso, wayne-enterprises, globex, wonka-industries, umbrella-health, initech. Contoso is the only affected account with a service-credit clause; the other six are on standard terms. Failed jobs are being re-driven; roughly 60% were done as of 13:52 with no failures.

**What to say on the board call:** There was a Batch API incident this morning. It was mitigated by 12:58 UTC and the service is recovering. We are monitoring before confirming recovery, and root cause is still being established. A further update will follow shortly. Do not describe the cause as identified or the incident as resolved — neither is true yet.

**Note:** a status-page update at 12:05 incorrectly stated the cause had been identified. That was a communications error; the cause is not confirmed. The 14:20 update corrects the public status to "Monitoring."

**Separately, unrelated to this incident:** During triage we found a database role over-permission (SEC-411). It played no part in this incident. Marcus owns the fix, targeted this week.
