## CEO BRIEFING

Dana, here is the state of play for your 15:00 board call.

**What happened:** At 11:42 UTC, the Batch API began returning elevated 502 errors on `POST /v1/batch/jobs`. The issue was confined to one internal worker fleet (fleet-a); the other fleet (fleet-b) was unaffected. Seven customer accounts on fleet-a experienced failures during the incident window (11:42–12:58). The affected accounts are: stark-industries, contoso, wayne-enterprises, globex, wonka-industries, umbrella-health, and initech. No other API surfaces were impacted.

**Mitigation:** At 12:58 UTC, all Batch API traffic was cut over to fleet-b. The error rate dropped to 0.4% within two minutes and has held at 0.3–0.4% since. A verification window is open until 14:30 UTC; we cannot declare the incident resolved before that closes cleanly.

**Root cause:** Not yet established. Two hypotheses remain under investigation: (1) a connection-pool change in release `r3` (max pool size lowered from 80 to 24 per worker) and (2) concentrated load on fleet-a from a large scheduled job. Neither has been tested. We are deliberately not rolling back `r3` yet to preserve evidence.

**Recovery:** Failed jobs are being re-driven for affected accounts; roughly 60% are complete with no failures on re-drive. No new customer failures have been reported since the cutover.

**SLA note:** Contoso is the only affected account with service-level credits in their contract. They have asked about credits; per contract, Monthly Availability is calculated after month end and published within 10 business days. We will assess then.

**Status page:** The 12:05 update incorrectly stated we had identified the cause. We are correcting that now with a new update.

I will provide a further update by 14:45 UTC, after the verification window closes.
