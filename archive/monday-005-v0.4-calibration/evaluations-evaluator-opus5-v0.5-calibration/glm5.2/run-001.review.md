## CEO BRIEFING

**INC-2026-1109-01 — state of play at 14:20 UTC, 9 November 2026**

**What happened**

At 11:42 UTC we saw elevated 502 errors on the Batch API, specifically on `POST /v1/batch/jobs`. The errors were confined to one of our two worker fleets (fleet-a); the other fleet (fleet-b) was unaffected throughout.

This followed a release at 11:31 (`batch-api@2026.11.09-r3`) that lowered the database connection-pool ceiling from 80 to 24 per worker. fleet-a was also running at roughly 3x its normal volume from 11:38, driven by a large scheduled job from one customer (wonka-industries). We have not confirmed which factor caused the failures — both remain untested hypotheses, and they may interact.

**What we did**

At 12:58 we cut all Batch API traffic to fleet-b and drained fleet-a. Error rate fell to 0.3–0.4% within two minutes (baseline is 0.2%). We deliberately did not roll back the release because doing so would have destroyed the evidence needed to determine the actual cause.

**Current state**

- Incident status: **OPEN** — mitigated, not resolved. Root cause not established.
- Verification window: open since 13:00, closes at 14:30. Our policy requires 90 minutes of continuous normal error rate before declaring resolved. At 14:20 we are at 0.3%, with 10 minutes remaining.
- fleet-a: still out of rotation, still broken, just not serving traffic.
- Re-drive of failed jobs: ~60% complete, no failures on re-drive.
- Marcus' internal planning estimate (not for external sharing): if the window closes clean and re-drive finishes, full wrap-up around 16:00.

**Who is hit**

Seven customer accounts, all on fleet-a, took failures during the incident window (11:42–12:58). Approximately **17,600 of 58,900** Batch API requests failed — a **~30% failure rate** for the Batch API during the window. For context, the Batch API was ~7% of platform-wide traffic (58,900 of 842,000 requests across all API surfaces). Other API surfaces (Inference, Embeddings, Files) were unaffected.

Per-account failures:

The two accounts on fleet-b (acme-corp, aperture-labs) had zero failures.

**Contoso / SLA exposure**

Contoso is the only affected account on a contract carrying service-level credits. They have written to support twice today — once reporting the 502s at 11:58, once asking about service credits at 12:26. I am sending them a response today with their impact figures and our SLA process.

Their contract (Schedule B) commits to 99.9% monthly availability across the Core APIs (Inference, Batch, Files). Credits apply if monthly availability falls below 99.9%: 10% at 99.0–99.9%, 25% at 95.0–99.0%, 50% below 95.0%. Their credit claim window opens after we publish the monthly figure. The other six affected accounts are on standard terms with no credit clause.

No update has been published since 12:05. I am publishing a corrected update now that reflects the actual state — mitigation applied, monitoring, next update by 15:00 UTC.
