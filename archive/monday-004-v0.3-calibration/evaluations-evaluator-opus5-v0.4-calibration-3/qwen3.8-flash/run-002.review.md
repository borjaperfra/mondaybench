## 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES

### 6.1 Duplicate invoice

`revenue.csv` contains:

```text
INV-2026-10-0209,soylent,2026-10-01,subscription,1,19500,stripe
INV-2026-10-0209,soylent,2026-10-02,subscription,1,19500,billing_sync
```

This violates the stated “one row per invoice” if `transaction_id` is unique. Treating it as a duplicate is the most reasonable interpretation, but leadership should confirm. If it is not a duplicate, October raw revenue is overstated by €19.5k.

### 6.2 Annual invoice timing

Gringotts’ October invoice is €120,000 for 12 months. Raw revenue recording puts the full amount in October. For run-rate health, this must be normalized to €10,000/month. Without deferred revenue schedules, recognized revenue cannot be precisely calculated.

### 6.3 Churned customers still contribute to October revenue

October invoices were issued early in the month. Customers who churned later in October still have October invoices. This makes October reported MRR higher than forward MRR.

### 6.4 Plan customer counts are not cleanly active-only

`plans.csv` October counts include churned customers. This affects:

- customers per plan
- average revenue per customer
- average requests per customer
- cost per customer
- contribution per customer

The plan-level revenue and cost are still useful, but active-only economics cannot be reliably recalculated from the supplied cost data.

### 6.5 Usage change is request-based, not value-based

`usage_change_pct` measures request change. Acme’s request decline is operationally explained, but tokens and active users are better proxies for consumption. Acme’s token decline was only 12%, and tokens per request rose sharply.

### 6.6 Acquisition attributed revenue is not collected revenue

`attributed_revenue_eur` is annualized contract value credited at conversion. It is not:

- cash collected
- recognized revenue
- current MRR
- guaranteed revenue
- net of churn

Expansion bookings take effect the following billing cycle, so October expansion bookings do not appear in October MRR.

### 6.7 Funnel conversions are paid trials, not paid customers

`funnel.csv` counts leads that reached a paid trial in the same month. It does not track trial-to-paid conversion. Therefore, funnel `conversions` cannot be directly compared to `acquisition.csv` conversions.

### 6.8 No channel cost data

CAC, CAC payback, channel ROI, and profitable growth cannot be calculated without spend.

### 6.9 No overage pricing

`included_requests_per_customer` is provided, but the price for usage beyond included requests is not. Therefore, potential overage revenue from Pro and Enterprise customers cannot be quantified.

### 6.10 Support cost is not purely ticket-driven

`support_cost_eur` includes fully loaded customer-success staff assigned to the plan. Ticket volume and support cost are related but not directly proportional. We cannot calculate cost per ticket reliably.

### 6.11 Small sample size

The pack covers only two complete months. Many conclusions depend on a small number of events:

- 5 churns
- 9 new logos
- 6 expansion bookings
- 2 months of usage
- 2 months of plan costs

This is enough for diagnosis, but not enough for strong causal claims or large budget reallocations.

### 6.12 No channel-to-churn linkage

The acquisition file does not include the original acquisition channel for the churned customers. We cannot determine whether churned customers came from partner, content, paid search, outbound, or another source.

## BOTTOM LINE

The leadership story should be:

1. **October’s raw revenue spike is not real growth.** It is mostly annual invoice timing, a possible duplicate, and advance invoices for customers who churned during October.
2. **Forward ARR is down 1.8%.** The business added new logos and one effective expansion, but churn and downgrade outweighed them.
3. **Usage is healthy.** Acme’s request drop is explained by batch processing; overall tokens and requests are growing.
4. **No acquisition channel should get more spend yet.** CAC payback cannot be calculated without spend and cohort conversion data.
5. **No customer segment should be cut purely from this pack.** SMB is messy but is the new-logo engine; Mid-market needs retention investigation; Enterprise needs margin/cost work.
6. **Pro deserves the next quarter’s engineering focus** because it has the best economics, growing usage, over-included usage, and visible rate-limit friction. Enterprise needs cost-efficiency attention because it is the largest revenue source but the biggest absolute margin risk.
