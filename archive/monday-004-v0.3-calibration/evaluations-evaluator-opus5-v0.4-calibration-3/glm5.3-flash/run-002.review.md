## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **October's headline revenue growth is an accounting artifact, not growth.** Invoiced revenue looks up ~12% MoM (€797.1k → €892.9k), but this includes a **€120k annual prepayment from gringotts booked entirely in October** and a **duplicated €19.5k soylent invoice** (same transaction ID loaded from two systems). Underlying monthly-equivalent recurring revenue grew just **+1.6%** (€746.1k → €757.7k) — and plan-level gross margin *fell* from 35.2% to 32.5%.
- **We lost 5 customers in October (−€303.6k ARR, 3.3% of opening ARR).** All five showed usage declines of −16% to −37% *before* churning. Usage decline is our most reliable churn early-warning signal — and it's flashing on **soylent** right now (−27% usage, a critical ticket, already downgraded €264k → €234k ARR).
- **Acme's −45% request drop is not a problem.** It matches the planned batch migration (40–50% estimate); tokens fell only 12%, so compute demand is largely retained.

Partner converts best (23–25%) but is tiny and lumpy (zero October conversions). **The CFO's CAC-payback request cannot be computed — there is no channel spend data in this pack.**
- **Plan economics:** Pro is the margin engine (63–68% gross margin) with visible unmet demand (rate-limit tickets doubled, all on pro; wonka running at 170% of included requests). Enterprise plan (87% of plan revenue) margin eroded 32.2% → 29.5% on rising support cost. Starter lost 44% of its September cohort.
- **Answers to the three decisions:** (1) Don't cut Enterprise or Mid-market; redirect SMB *acquisition* spend toward retention rather than cutting the segment outright. (2) Do not increase channel spend on this evidence — fix measurement first. (3) Pro plan gets product's focus, with a parallel reliability/cost-to-serve intervention on the enterprise plan.

7. **Support data gaps:** no timestamps or resolution durations (SLA not computable); status is a point-in-time snapshot. Also, support_cost moves don't track ticket counts (starter +41% cost on +2 tickets) — the CS allocation methodology needs checking before we draw cost conclusions from it.
