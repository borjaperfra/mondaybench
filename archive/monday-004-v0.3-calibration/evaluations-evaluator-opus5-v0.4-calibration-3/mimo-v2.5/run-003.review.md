### 4.5 Verdict: Which Plan Deserves Engineering Focus?

**The Pro plan.** Three reasons:

1. **Highest margin, most growth potential.** Pro generates 62.7% gross margin vs. 29.5% for enterprise and 32.2% for starter. Every incremental Pro customer or retention avoided is worth €4,611/month in margin.

2. **Product friction is concentrated here.** Wonka filed 8 rate-limit tickets in October. Aperture filed 2. Prestige has 2. The 600K included limit is being breached by a third of the customer base. Engineering work to raise included allocations, improve rate-limit messaging, or smooth overage billing would directly reduce support load and churn risk.

3. **Usage growth is strongest.** Vandelay +32.7%, Aperture +21.6%, Hooli +22.2% (enterprise-plan mid-market, but similar profile). These customers are growing into the platform and hitting plan ceilings. If Northstar doesn't accommodate that growth, they risk losing them to competitors or forcing painful plan negotiations.

The enterprise plan's declining margin (32.2% → 29.5%) warrants investigation — likely rising support cost per customer given the Soylent situation — but is primarily a cost-management and account-health issue, not a product-engineering issue.
