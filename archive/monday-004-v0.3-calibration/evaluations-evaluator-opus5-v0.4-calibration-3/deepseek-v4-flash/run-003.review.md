## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **October headline revenue looks higher, but the underlying business did not grow.** October invoiced revenue was boosted by one annual invoice (Gringotts, €120k), professional services, and the fact that October invoices were issued before several customers churned.
- **Underlying MRR declined.** Plans MRR was €746.1k in September and €757.7k in October, but October includes €25.3k MRR from customers who churned during the month. Exiting October, recurring MRR is roughly **€732.4k, down ~1.8%**.
- **SMB is the weakest segment.** 4 of 5 October churns were SMB; SMB ARR churn was ~20.5% in one month. New SMB logos barely offset the churn.
- **Paid search looks strong only because of expansion bookings, not new customer acquisition.** Its funnel conversion rate actually declined.
- **The Pro plan is the most profitable by margin but is showing rate-limit stress.** Enterprise is the largest profit pool, but its contribution margin is eroding.

## 5. RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. Reduce incremental investment in SMB

The data supports reducing new SMB acquisition spend until retention improves. SMB had ~20.5% ARR churn in one month, and new SMB logos barely offset churn. Existing SMB customers should still be supported, but adding more SMB spend is not supported by the data.

### 2. Increase spend on content, not paid search

Content is the only channel with consistent new-logo output and stable conversion. Paid search looks strong only because of expansion bookings, and its new-logo conversion rate declined. Before scaling any channel, the Growth team should get channel spend data so CAC payback can actually be calculated.

### 3. Give the Pro plan the next engineering focus

Pro is the highest-margin plan and is growing, but it is showing rate-limit and overage stress. Three of nine Pro customers exceed included request volume, and rate-limit tickets more than doubled. Fixing Pro usage limits and packaging protects the most profitable plan.

Enterprise should be watched closely because its contribution margin is eroding, but the more urgent product signal is in Pro.

## 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES

- **Duplicate Soylent invoice**: `INV-2026-10-0209` appears twice — once from Stripe and once from billing_sync. If not deduplicated, October revenue is overstated by €19.5k.
- **Gringotts annual invoice**: the €120k invoice is cash revenue, not MRR. The plans table correctly treats it as €10k/month.
- **October invoices include churned customers**: invoices were issued October 1–3, before churn dates later in October. This overstates October recurring revenue.
- **Acquisition attributed revenue includes expansions**: expansion bookings take effect in later billing cycles, so they are not yet in revenue.
- **Funnel data only tracks leads to paid trial**, not trial-to-paid. We cannot calculate true channel ROI from funnel conversion alone.
- **No marketing spend data**: CAC payback by channel cannot be calculated reliably.
- **Support ticket counts are not cost-weighted**: support_cost_eur is an allocation, not a per-ticket cost.
- **Usage averages hide overage distribution**: some individual customers exceed included request volumes even when plan averages look safe.
- **Acme’s request decline is expected** due to the batch migration; it should not be read as churn risk.
