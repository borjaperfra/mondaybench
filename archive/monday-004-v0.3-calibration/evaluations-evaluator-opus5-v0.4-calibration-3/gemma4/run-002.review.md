## Full response

To: Northstar Leadership Team
From: Product & Growth Lead
Date: Monday, November 2026

### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The business is experiencing a divergence between top-line revenue growth and underlying customer health. While total revenue is bolstered by a large annual contract from Gringotts, **Enterprise segment health is declining** due to a significant usage drop from a major client (Acme-corp) and rising support friction. **Acquisition performance is highly skewed**: Paid Search is driving the highest volume of leads and conversions, but its efficiency (CAC payback) is unproven compared to the high-value, low-volume Partner channel. Regarding product focus, the **Enterprise plan is the most economically efficient**, despite high absolute costs, while the **Starter plan is currently a loss-leader** with high relative support costs.

---

### 2. REVENUE & CUSTOMER HEALTH
*   **Revenue Drivers:** October revenue is heavily influenced by a €120k annual invoice from Gringotts. Excluding this one-time cash event, monthly recurring revenue (MRR) remains relatively stable.
*   **The "Acme" Risk:** A critical finding is the 45% drop in Acme-corp’s request volume in October. While the operational notes suggest this is due to a shift to batch processing (which is expected), it represents a significant change in how our largest customer consumes the platform.
*   **Segment Health:** 
    *   **Enterprise:** High revenue, but seeing "High" severity API and Performance tickets.
    *   **Mid-Market:** Stable, but showing signs of churn (Nakatomi).
    *   **SMB:** High churn rate in October (4 customers churned: Slate-rock, Mega-lo-mart, Dinoco, Buy-n-large).

---

### 3. ACQUISITION
**Channel Performance Analysis:**

| Channel | Sept Leads | Sept Conv. | Sept Conv % | Oct Leads | Oct Conv. | Oct Conv % |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paid Search** | 520 | 44 | 8.5% | 900 | 58 | 6.4% |
| **Content** | 260 | 31 | 12.0% | 330 | 34 | 10.3% |
| **Partner** | 60 | 15 | 25.0% | 75 | 17 | 22.7% |
| **Outbound** | 160 | 10 | 6.3% | 195 | 11 | 5.6% |

*   **What can be concluded:** **Partner** is the most efficient channel for conversion. **Paid Search** is the primary engine for volume/scale.
*   **What cannot be concluded:** We cannot determine which channel to "increase spend" on definitively because **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) is missing**. While we have "Attributed Revenue," we do not have the "Spend" per channel. We cannot calculate the CFO's requested **CAC Payback** without the marketing spend data.

---

### 4. PLAN ECONOMICS (October Data)
We must look at the margin (Revenue - Infra - Support) to determine true plan strength.

| Plan | Revenue | Infra Cost | Support Cost | **Gross Margin (EUR)** | **Margin %** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise** | €659,500 | €296,800 | €168,000 | **€194,700** | **29.5%** |
| **Pro** | €66,200 | €16,100 | €8,600 | **€41,500** | **62.7%** |
| **Starter** | €32,000 | €11,400 | €10,300 | **€10,300** | **32.2%** |

*   **Strongest Plan:** The **Pro** plan is the most economically robust, boasting a 62.7% margin.
*   **Weakest Plan:** The **Starter** plan has the lowest absolute margin and a high support-to-revenue ratio (Support costs are ~32% of its revenue), making it the most sensitive to scaling issues.

---

### 5. RECOMMENDATIONS
1.  **Finance (Segment Investment):** **Do not reduce investment in Enterprise yet.** While Acme's usage dropped, it was a planned transition to batching, not a loss of utility. However, monitor the "High" severity tickets closely to ensure the transition doesn't lead to churn.
2.  **Growth (Acquisition Spend):** **Hold on increasing spend** until Marketing provides the "Spend" figures. We need to calculate the ROI/Payback. If the goal is pure volume, Paid Search is the lever; if the goal is efficiency, Partner is the lever.
3.  **Product (Engineering Focus):** **Focus on the Pro plan.** It has the best unit economics and provides the best balance of scale and margin. Alternatively, if the goal is to protect the bottom line, engineering should focus on **automating support/onboarding for the Starter plan** to reduce its high support-cost ratio.

---

### 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES
*   **Missing CAC Data:** We cannot fulfill the CFO's request for "CAC Payback" or make a data-driven decision on acquisition spend without knowing the cost of the leads.
*   **Attribution Bias:** The `acquisition.csv` file notes that "Expansion bookings take effect from the following billing cycle." This means current attributed revenue may underrepresent the true long-term value of certain channels.
*   **Acme Usage:** While the operational note explains the 45% drop, we cannot verify if the "batch runs" will result in lower *token* usage or just lower *request* counts without seeing the `tokens_m` trend for Acme specifically (though `tokens_m` did drop from 4620 to 4066, which is a ~12% drop, suggesting the batching is indeed more efficient).
*   **Support Status:** A significant number of "High" and "Medium" severity tickets are "Open" or "Pending." This represents a growing operational debt that could impact retention.
