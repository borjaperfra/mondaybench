- **Enterprise plan has the lowest margin and is eroding** (32.2% → 29.5%); Pro has the best margin (~63%) but active revenue is declining; Starter has low margin and high churn.  
- **CAC payback cannot be calculated** — no marketing spend data is provided.

## 5. RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. Customer segment investment

**Supported: Reduce investment in SMB Starter acquisition, not the entire SMB segment.**

- All 4 churned customers were SMB Starter.
- Active Starter MRR declined from €24.1k to €21.7k.
- New Starter customers have lower ACV (~€1.98k) than churned ones (~€2.58k).
- SMB Pro customers are profitable and should be retained/upsold.

**Not supported:** Reducing investment in Enterprise (stable, high revenue) or Mid-market as a whole (one large churn, but high-value accounts).

### 2. Acquisition channel spend

**Supported: Increase spend on the partner channel, with caution.**

- Partner has the highest trial conversion rate (~23–25%).
- In September, partner produced two new logos worth €166.8k attributed revenue.
- October partner trial conversions are strong (17), but paid conversions have not yet appeared — likely due to trial-to-paid lag.

**Not supported:** Increasing paid search spend based on October revenue, because most of that revenue is from expansions, not new logos, and its conversion rate is lowest.

**Cannot conclude:** CAC payback — no cost data.

### 3. Product plan engineering focus

**Enterprise plan deserves the most attention.**

- It generates the majority of revenue and contribution.
- Its margin fell from 32.2% to 29.5% due to rising infra and support costs.
- A small improvement in enterprise cost efficiency has outsized impact.

**Secondary focus:** Pro plan retention, because it has the best margin but is losing active revenue.
