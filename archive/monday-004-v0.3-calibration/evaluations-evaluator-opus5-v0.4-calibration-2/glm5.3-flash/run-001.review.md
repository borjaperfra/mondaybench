# Northstar — Monday Business Review: Data Read & Decision Support

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- **The headline revenue "growth" is an illusion this month.** October invoiced revenue looks up ~9.6% vs September (€873.4k vs €797.1k, after removing one duplicate), but ~€120k of that is a 12-month annual prepayment (gringotts), ~€19.5k is a duplicated invoice, and €25.3k is advance billing to customers who churned mid-October. Underlying recurring revenue grew only **+1.6%** (€746.1k → €757.7k monthly-equivalent), and the **November exit run-rate is ~€732.4k — below September's level**.
- **Churn is real but concentrated and predictable.** 5 customers (€303.6k ARR) churned in October. Four were SMB/starter accounts whose usage fell ~35% during the month before churning — a visible early-warning signal we failed to act on. The fifth (nakatomi, €180k, mid-market) had an open high-severity API ticket when it churned.
- **Top retention risk right now: soylent** — usage −27%, invoice already cut from €22.0k to €19.5k/mo, 8 tickets in October including the pack's only critical severity.
- **Do not scale paid_search on current evidence.** Its €274.8k attributed revenue is 76% expansion credits from existing customers. Its new-logo output is 2 logos / €64.8k from 1,420 leads, and lead→trial conversion *fell* from 8.5% to 6.4% as lead volume rose 73%.
- **No segment cut is supported by the data** — the naive target (SMB) is actually net-growing. Details in §2 and §5.
- **CAC payback by channel (CFO ask) cannot be calculated from this pack.** No channel spend data exists here, and trial-to-paid conversion is not tracked.

## 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES

1. **Duplicate invoice:** INV-2026-10-0209 (soylent, €19,500) appears twice with different sources (stripe 10-01, billing_sync 10-02). Naive October revenue is overstated by €19.5k.
2. **Prepayment distortion:** gringotts' €120k annual invoice makes October look like +9.6% growth; plans.csv correctly counts it as €10k/month.
3. **Advance billing of churners:** €25.3k of October revenue is from customers who churned mid-month; November will mechanically underperform October.
4. **CAC payback not calculable:** no channel spend data; funnel "conversions" are trial starts, not sales; trial-to-paid untracked; `attributed_revenue_eur` includes expansion credits (76% of paid_search's total).
5. **Attribution causality is unsupported:** years-old enterprise customers' expansions credited to channels via campaigns that started weeks earlier — treat as correlation.
6. **Two months of data, small n:** partner = 2 logos (one dominant); mid-market "downtrend" = 2 events. No seasonality baseline.
7. **Segment-level margin not computable:** costs exist only at plan level, and mid-market spans both enterprise and pro plans.
8. **plans.csv October counts include mid-month churners** (nakatomi still in pro's 9), so per-customer metrics are slightly off.
9. **Acme overage impact unquantifiable:** overage rate (€ per request beyond included) is not provided.
10. **Rate-limit ticket anomaly:** some come from accounts far below their monthly caps — either burst throttling (not in data) or miscategorisation; investigate before productising.
11. **No causal claim is made** that support failures caused the churns (nakatomi, soylent patterns are suggestive, not proven); and Acme's usage drop must not be read as disengagement — it is documented migration behaviour.
