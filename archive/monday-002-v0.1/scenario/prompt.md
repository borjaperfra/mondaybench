You are returning to work on Monday morning after being offline since Wednesday at 17:30.

You are the Product & Growth Lead at Northstar, a fictional B2B SaaS company.

Below are the Slack messages, emails, meeting notes, task list, deployment data, and account data that accumulated while you were away.

Your job is to reconstruct the CURRENT state of the situation, not simply summarize what was said.

Produce a concise Monday briefing with exactly these sections:

1. CURRENT STATE
   - What is true now?
   - Where information changed over time, use the latest valid information and explain the change only when it matters.

2. WHAT I NEED TO DO TODAY
   - Ordered by priority.
   - Distinguish actions I personally own from actions I need to unblock, verify, or coordinate with someone else.
   - Do not assign me work that has been reassigned to another owner.

3. BLOCKERS & DEPENDENCIES
   - Identify the actual current blocker(s).
   - If several steps depend on one another, give the dependency order.
   - Do not treat superseded blockers as current.

4. IMPORTANT, BUT NO ACTION FROM ME
   - Include resolved, reassigned, completed, or informational items that matter but do not require my action.

5. UNCLEAR / MUST VERIFY
   - Explicitly identify conflicts or missing information that cannot be resolved from the provided sources.
   - Say what must be verified before acting.
   - Do not guess.

Be concise and practical. Do not assume information that is not provided.
