# MondayBench #002 — Moving Targets

**Status: calibration draft**

> Can a model figure out what's still true after decisions, blockers and ownership changed?

#002 complements #001. It tests:
- superseded instructions;
- stale urgent tasks;
- changing owners;
- dependent release gates;
- misleading "final" artifacts;
- genuinely unresolved evidence;
- business-data reconciliation.

## Scoring
100 base points:
- Current-state reconstruction — 30
- Supersession reasoning — 20
- Action selection — 20
- Dependency/blocker reasoning — 15
- Uncertainty — 10
- Data reasoning — 5

Global penalties are separate, capped at 25.

Noise is not a standalone dimension here. The dangerous noise is plausible but stale professional information.

Do not publish as official until calibrated and frozen.
