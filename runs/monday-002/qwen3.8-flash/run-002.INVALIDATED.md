## Why it stopped counting

Not because the score is low, and not because the response is bad. Because it
was served outside the reasoning regime its siblings used, so averaging the
three would report a mixture of two different systems as one model's
variability. `qualify_run` now rejects this automatically, from the response
rather than from a flag anyone set.
