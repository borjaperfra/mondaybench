**Evidence and hypotheses**
- **H1, leading but untested:** Reduced connection-pool ceiling. Supporting evidence: deployment preceded the alert by 11 minutes; affected workers were waiting on database connections. Counterevidence: fleet-b runs the same release without degradation.
- **H2, live but untested:** Concentrated load. fleet-a reached approximately three times its morning average following Wonka’s scheduled job. Counterevidence: it handled a higher peak on 3 November.
- **Interaction:** Lower pool capacity under higher concurrency is plausible, but also untested.
- Upstream storage was ruled out at 11:47: provider status was healthy and Files, using the same provider, remained healthy. Shared gateway/client SDK were also ruled out in channel.
- The rollback was deliberately deferred to preserve evidence and avoid confusing release effects with load effects. Successful traffic movement is mitigation evidence, not proof of cause.
