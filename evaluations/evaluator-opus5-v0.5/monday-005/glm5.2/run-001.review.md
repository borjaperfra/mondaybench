**INC-2026-1109-01 — Batch API incident, 9 November 2026**
Prepared 14:20 UTC for your 15:00 board call.

**State of play**
OPEN: mitigated, verification in progress. Root cause not established.

- **11:42** — 502 errors began on the Batch API (`POST /v1/batch/jobs`), confined to fleet-a (one of two internal worker fleets). fleet-b was unaffected.
- **12:58** — Mitigation applied: all Batch API traffic moved to fleet-b, fleet-a drained. Error rate back to baseline (~0.3%) within minutes, holding steady.
- **13:00–14:30** — Verification window (90 min of clean error rate required before declaring resolved). At 14:12 the rate was 0.3%; 18 minutes remain.

**What happened**
Release `batch-api@2026.11.09-r3` shipped at 11:31 to both fleets. It lowered the database connection-pool ceiling from 80 to 24 per worker. Eleven minutes later, fleet-a began throwing 502s under elevated load. fleet-b, running the same release, was fine.

**Root cause — not established**
Two hypotheses, neither tested:

1. **Pool-size change in r3 (leading).** Timing fits; fleet-a workers were queueing on DB connections. But r3 also deployed to fleet-b without issue, so it doesn't explain why only fleet-a degraded.
2. **Concentrated load on fleet-a (secondary).** fleet-a was at ~3x its morning average from 11:38, driven by one customer's large scheduled job. But fleet-a absorbed a higher peak on 3 November without errors.

We chose to mitigate (move traffic) rather than roll back r3, to preserve fleet-a's state for root-cause testing. The rollback test has not been run.

**Impact**
- 7 of 9 Batch API accounts took failures, all on fleet-a. 2 fleet-b accounts were unaffected.
- **17,602 failed Batch API requests out of 58,900 total** in the window (11:42–12:58) — ~29.9% of Batch API traffic.
- Other API surfaces (Inference, Embeddings, Files) were not affected.
- Re-drive of failed jobs ~60% complete; no failures on re-drive.

**Contoso**
The only affected account with service-level credits in their contract. They wrote to support twice today — reporting the 502s, then asking about service credits under Schedule B. Their contract commits to 99.9% monthly availability; the determination is made after month-end from our records, so we cannot confirm a breach today. I am replying to them now with their impact figures (9,400 requests, 3,410 failures, 11:43–12:56) and the SLA process.

**Comms issue**

**For the board call**
