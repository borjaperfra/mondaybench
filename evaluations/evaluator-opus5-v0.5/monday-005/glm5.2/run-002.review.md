## CEO BRIEFING

**State of play at 14:20 UTC — INC-2026-1109-01**

**One-line summary:** The Batch API had elevated 502 errors from 11:42 to 12:58. We mitigated by moving all Batch traffic to the healthy fleet at 12:58. Error rate is back to baseline (0.3%). The incident is still formally OPEN — a 90-minute verification window closes at 14:30, and root cause is not yet established.

**What happened**
- Release `batch-api@2026.11.09-r3` shipped at 11:31 to both worker fleets. It included a database connection-pool change (max pool size 80 → 24 per worker).
- At 11:38 a large scheduled job from wonka-industries drove fleet-a volume to ~3x its morning average.
- At 11:42, 502 errors on `POST /v1/batch/jobs` crossed the page threshold on fleet-a. fleet-b was and remained healthy throughout.
- At 12:58 we cut all Batch API traffic to fleet-b and drained fleet-a. Error rate fell to 0.4% within two minutes and is holding at 0.3% (baseline 0.2%).

**Impact (incident window 11:42–12:58)**
- Headline: **17,602 failed Batch API requests out of 58,900 total — ~29.9% failure rate** on that API surface during the window.
- 7 customer accounts took failures, all on fleet-a. 2 fleet-b accounts were unaffected. No other API surface (Inference, Embeddings, Files) was affected.
- Per-account figures are in `customer-impact.csv`.
- **Contoso** is the only affected account with an SLA credit clause. They have written twice today asking about service credits. Their contract determines availability monthly, after month end, and credits are claimed after publication — so we cannot confirm a breach or a credit amount now. Their window figures: 9,400 requests, 3,410 failures, first failure 11:43, last 12:56.

**What is still open**
- Root cause not established. Two untested hypotheses: (H1, leading) the pool-size change in r3; (H2, secondary) concentrated load on fleet-a. They may interact. The rollback test has not been run.
- fleet-a is still out of rotation.
- Re-drive of failed jobs is ~60% complete; no failures on re-drive so far.
- Verification window closes at 14:30. Nothing is called resolved before then.

**What to say if it comes up on the board call**

The only customer with an SLA credit clause has been contacted and their figures shared; any credit is determined after month end per contract."

**Two things to be aware of**

Marcus corrected this internally at 12:09. No correction has been published yet. The next status update (being published now) will reflect the accurate state.
