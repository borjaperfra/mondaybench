**Executive Summary**
At 11:42 UTC today, the Batch API experienced elevated error rates (502s) affecting 7 customer accounts. The root cause was a database connection pool configuration change in release `r3` deployed at 11:31 UTC, which caused connection exhaustion under high load on one of our two worker fleets (fleet-a).

**Current Status**
*   **Mitigated:** At 12:58 UTC, all Batch API traffic was successfully shifted to fleet-b, which remained healthy. Error rates have returned to baseline (0.3–0.4%).
*   **Verification:** We are currently in a mandatory 90-minute verification window (ending 14:30 UTC) to confirm stability before declaring the incident resolved.
*   **Recovery:** Failed jobs are being re-driven. Approximately 60% of affected jobs have been successfully re-processed with no new failures.

**Impact**
*   **Scope:** 7 out of 9 Batch API customer accounts were affected. All were on fleet-a.
*   **Severity:** No data loss. Service was degraded (partial failures), not down.
*   **Financial/Contractual:** One affected customer, Contoso, has an SLA contract. Their monthly availability is likely impacted, triggering potential service credits. We are preparing to address this formally.

**Key Messages for Board**
1.  **Response was effective:** The engineering team quickly identified the issue, isolated the affected fleet, and restored service within ~1.5 hours.
2.  **Root Cause Identified:** The issue was a configuration change (connection pool size) that interacted poorly with a spike in traffic from one customer (Wonka Industries). It was not a security breach or upstream provider failure.
3.  **No Systemic Risk:** The other fleet (fleet-b) was unaffected, demonstrating our multi-fleet architecture worked as intended.
4.  **Next Steps:** Incident will be declared resolved at 14:30 UTC. We will conduct a post-mortem and implement safeguards to prevent similar configuration-induced issues. Contoso will be contacted regarding SLA credits.

**Immediate Actions**

**Incident:** INC-2026-1109-01

**Summary**
The Batch API incident affecting fleet-a has been mitigated by shifting all traffic to fleet-b at 12:58 UTC. Error rates are stable at 0.3–0.4% (baseline 0.2%). We are in the 90-minute verification window, which closes at 14:30 UTC.

**Root Cause Analysis (Preliminary)**
*   **Primary Candidate (H1):** Release `r3` (deployed 11:31 UTC) lowered the database connection pool max size from 80 to 24 per worker. This coincided with a 3x traffic spike on fleet-a (driven by Wonka Industries' scheduled job starting at 11:38 UTC). Workers on fleet-a queued on database connections, leading to 502 errors.
*   **Secondary Candidate (H2):** The traffic spike itself may have been a contributing factor, though fleet-a handled higher peaks previously.
*   **Note:** The pool change was also deployed to fleet-b, which remained healthy. This suggests fleet-a may have had different underlying conditions (e.g., database latency, connection leaks) that made it more susceptible. This is under investigation.
*   **Upstream Provider:** Ruled out (Files API healthy, provider status green).
