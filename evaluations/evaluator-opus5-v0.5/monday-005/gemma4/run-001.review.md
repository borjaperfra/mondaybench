## CEO BRIEFING

At 11:42 UTC, we began experiencing elevated error rates (502 responses) specifically on the Batch API. The issue was confined to one of our two worker fleets (fleet-a).

We applied a mitigation at 12:58 by moving all Batch API traffic to the healthy fleet (fleet-b). Since the cutover, error rates have dropped significantly and are currently holding at approximately 0.3%.

The root cause is not yet established. We are investigating two main hypotheses: a recent change to database connection pool settings in the latest release, or concentrated load on the affected fleet. We have not yet performed a rollback test to confirm.

The incident is currently in a verification window that ends at 14:30 UTC. We are also re-driving failed jobs for affected accounts; roughly 60% have been processed so far with no further failures.

Impact: Seven customer accounts were affected by the failures on fleet-a. Contoso is the only affected customer with an SLA that carries service credits.

For your 15:00 board call: The service is mitigated and recovering, but we are still in the monitoring phase to confirm full stability.
