## CEO BRIEFING

Dana,

Here's the state of play before your 15:00 call.

**What happened.** A routine release shipped at 11:31 UTC today changed a database connection-pool setting on our Batch API. Eleven minutes later, a large scheduled job from one customer drove traffic on one of our two worker fleets to roughly three times normal. The combination produced a wave of 502 errors on Batch API job submissions. The other fleet was unaffected throughout.

**Impact.** Seven of our nine Batch API customers saw request failures between 11:42 and 12:58 UTC. Approximately 17,600 of 58,900 Batch API requests in that window returned errors — roughly 30%. Other Northstar API surfaces (Inference, Embeddings, Files) were not affected.

**Mitigation.** At 12:58 UTC we moved all Batch API traffic to the unaffected fleet. Error rates returned to near-baseline within two minutes and have held stable since. We are in a 90-minute monitoring window that closes at 14:30 UTC. If it closes clean, we will declare the incident resolved.

**Root cause.** Two hypotheses remain live and neither has been tested yet. We deliberately held back the rollback to preserve evidence for testing. Confirmation will follow after the monitoring window.

**Commercial exposure.** One affected customer — Contoso — has an SLA contract with service-credit provisions. Monthly availability is calculated at the end of November per the contract; no credit determination is due today. The other six affected customers are on standard terms.

**If it comes up on your call.** "We had a Batch API incident this morning that affected some customers. We mitigated it before 13:00 UTC and the service has been stable since. We're in our monitoring window now and will confirm resolution once it closes. Root-cause work is underway."
