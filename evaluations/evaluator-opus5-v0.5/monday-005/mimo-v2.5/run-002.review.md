Contoso is the only affected customer whose contract carries service-level credits.

**Outstanding items.**
- Verification window closes 14:30 UTC; resolution cannot be stated before then.
- Failed jobs from the window are being re-driven; roughly 60% complete with no re-drive failures so far.

- Multiple customers have contacted support. Contoso has raised two tickets, including a question about service credits under their Schedule B SLA.

Their window to submit a claim is 30 days after publication.

**What to say if it comes up at the board.**
