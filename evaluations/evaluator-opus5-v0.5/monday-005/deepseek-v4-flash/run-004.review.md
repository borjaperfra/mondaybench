Dana, for your 15:00 board call:

**What happened**
- Incident on the Batch API, started 11:42 UTC. Elevated 502 errors on job submissions. Other API surfaces (Inference, Embeddings, Files) unaffected.

- Upstream storage provider ruled out. Root cause not yet established. Two hypotheses remain: a database connection-pool change in r3, and concentrated load. Neither has been tested.

**Who is hit**
- Seven customer accounts took failures, all on fleet-a: stark-industries, contoso, wayne-enterprises, globex, wonka-industries, umbrella-health, initech.

- Contoso is the only affected account with a service-credit clause. They have asked about credits; per their contract, monthly availability is determined after month end, so we cannot confirm a breach or credit amount now.

**Mitigation and current status**
- At 12:58 UTC, all Batch API traffic was moved to the healthy fleet (fleet-b). Error rate fell to 0.4% within two minutes; now 0.3% (baseline 0.2%).
- Verification window open until 14:30. Incident cannot be declared resolved before then. Re-drive of failed jobs ~60% complete, no failures on re-drive.
- We deliberately did not roll back r3, to preserve evidence for root-cause testing.
