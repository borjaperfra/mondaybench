## CEO BRIEFING

Dana, state of play for your 15:00 board call.

**What happened**
- At 11:42 UTC the Batch API began returning elevated 502 errors on `POST /v1/batch/jobs`. The incident is confined to the Batch API; Inference, Embeddings and Files are unaffected.
- A small release (`batch-api@2026.11.09-r3`) went out at 11:31 and changed database connection-pool settings. Around the same time, a large scheduled job from one customer (wonka-industries) drove traffic on the affected fleet to roughly 3x its morning average. Both are under investigation; neither is confirmed as the cause. The upstream storage provider was ruled out.
- At 12:58 UTC we applied a mitigation: all Batch API traffic was moved to the healthy fleet. Error rates fell to 0.4% within two minutes and are now 0.3% (baseline 0.2%).

**Who is hit**
- 7 of the 9 Batch API accounts took failures: stark-industries, contoso, wayne-enterprises, globex, wonka-industries, umbrella-health and initech. All are on the affected internal fleet.
- In the incident window (11:42–12:58), the Batch API served 58,900 requests, of which 17,602 failed — 29.9%. Platform-wide we served 842,000 requests, so this is a small, isolated part of the platform.
- Contoso is the only affected account with a service-credit clause. They have asked about credits; we have responded per their contract — the monthly availability figure is calculated after month end.

**Status**
- The incident is OPEN. It is mitigated and we are in a verification window that closes at 14:30. Root cause is not yet established. The affected fleet is out of rotation. Re-drive of failed jobs is ~60% complete with no failures.
- Note: the 12:05 status-page post said the cause was identified; that was premature and the page is being corrected.

**What to say if it comes up**
- "We had an incident this morning affecting a subset of Batch API requests. It was mitigated and the service is recovering. We are monitoring before confirming recovery, and root cause is still being confirmed." Do not say resolved, do not name internal fleet details, do not attribute cause.

Next status-page update will go out by 15:00 UTC.
