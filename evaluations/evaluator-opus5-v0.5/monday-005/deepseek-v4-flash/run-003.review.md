## CEO BRIEFING

Dana — state of play for your 15:00 board call.

**What happened**
- At 11:42 UTC, the Batch API began returning elevated 502 errors on `POST /v1/batch/jobs`. Incident INC-2026-1109-01.

- A release deployed at 11:31 (`batch-api@2026.11.09-r3`) lowered the database connection-pool ceiling from 80 to 24 per worker. It is the leading hypothesis but is not confirmed. Heavy load on fleet-a (about 3x the morning average, driven by wonka-industries' scheduled job) is also still a candidate. Root cause is not established.
- Mitigation applied at 12:58 UTC: all Batch API traffic moved to fleet-b. Error rate fell to 0.4% within two minutes and is holding at 0.3% (baseline 0.2%).

**Who is hit**
- In the incident window (11:42–12:58), the Batch API served 58,900 requests across the 9 accounts that use it; 17,602 failed — 29.9% of Batch API requests in the window.

- Contoso is the only affected account with a service-credit clause; they have asked about credits and we are responding per their contract (monthly availability is calculated after month end, so no breach or credit can be confirmed now).
- No other API surfaces (Inference, Embeddings, Files) were affected. Batch API is a small share of the 842,000 platform-wide requests in the window.
- Affected customers have been in touch; their tickets are being handled and failed jobs are being re-driven.

**Current status**
- Incident is OPEN but mitigated. Verification window opened at 13:00 and closes at 14:30; we need 90 minutes of normal error rate before declaring resolved. At 14:12 the rate was 0.3%.
- Root cause not established; the rollback test has not been run. fleet-a is out of rotation. Re-drive of failed jobs is ~60% complete with no failures.
- Marcus's internal planning estimate: full wrap-up and fleet-a back in rotation around 16:00. Not for external use.
