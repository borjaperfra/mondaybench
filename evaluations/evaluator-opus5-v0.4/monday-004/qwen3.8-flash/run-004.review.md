## 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES

### 6.1 Duplicate invoice

`revenue.csv` contains:

```text
INV-2026-10-0209,soylent,2026-10-01,subscription,1,19500,stripe
INV-2026-10-0209,soylent,2026-10-02,subscription,1,19500,billing_sync
```

Same transaction ID, customer, amount, and invoice type. This appears to be a duplicate load. If not removed, October revenue is overstated by **19,500**.

### 6.2 Annual invoice distorts raw revenue

Gringotts has:

```text
INV-2026-10-0236,gringotts,2026-10-01,annual_subscription,12,120000,stripe
```

Cash invoiced: **120,000**  
Monthly recurring equivalent: **10,000**

Raw October invoice revenue is therefore not comparable to September MRR.

### 6.3 Churned customers are still invoiced in October

October invoices include:

- Nakatomi: 15,000
- Slate Rock: 2,000
- Mega-Lou Mart: 2,700
- Dinoco: 2,300
- Buy-N-Large: 3,300

Total churned October subscription invoices: **25,300**

This inflates October recurring revenue if not adjusted.

### 6.4 `arr_eur` includes churned customers

`customers.csv` includes churned customers with non-zero `arr_eur`.

Total ARR if all rows are summed: **9,092,400**  
Total active ARR: **8,788,800**

Difference: **303,600**

Leadership must filter by `status = active` for current ARR.

### 6.5 ARR and invoice mismatches

Some current ARR values do not match September invoices:

| Customer | September invoice | October invoice | Current ARR | Monthly ARR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hooli | 18,000 | 21,000 | 252,000 | 21,000 |
| Soylent | 22,000 | 19,500 | 234,000 | 19,500 |

Hooli is consistent with a September expansion effective in October. Soylent needs clarification: it may be a downsize, correction, or data issue.

### 6.6 Expansion timing is not reflected in October invoices

October expansion bookings include:

- Stark Industries: 84,000 ARR
- Umbrella Health: 48,000 ARR
- Globex: 42,000 ARR
- Massive Dynamic: 18,000 ARR
- Cyberdyne: 24,000 ARR

Total: **216,000 ARR = 18,000 MRR**

These are not visible in October invoices because expansion bookings take effect from the following billing cycle.

### 6.7 Funnel and acquisition data do not reconcile

Funnel October paid trials:

- Paid_search: 58
- Content: 34
- Partner: 17
- Outbound: 11
- Total: **120**

Acquisition October rows:

- 9 total rows, including new logos and expansions

There is no lead ID in `funnel.csv`, and trial-to-paid conversion is not tracked. Therefore, paid-trial counts cannot be converted into paid-customer counts or channel revenue.

### 6.8 No channel spend

CAC payback cannot be calculated because there is no spend data by channel.

### 6.9 Request volume is not a reliable compute proxy

Acme’s requests fell **45%**, but tokens fell only **12%**. This is consistent with the operational note about batch migration.

For cost and usage health, tokens may be more relevant than request count, but the supplied data does not include a direct cost-per-token model.

### 6.10 Overage revenue cannot be calculated

The plan file says usage beyond included requests is billed separately at renewal. But no overage price is supplied.

Therefore, Pro customers above included requests cannot be monetized in this analysis.

### 6.11 Plan costs are not allocated by customer segment

`plans.csv` gives costs by plan, not by segment. Mid-market and SMB span multiple plans, so segment-level cost allocation requires assumptions.

The segment contribution estimates in this memo are directional only.

### 6.12 Support cost includes fixed customer-success staffing

`support_cost_eur` includes fully loaded cost of customer-success staff assigned to the plan. Therefore, support cost changes are not purely ticket-volume-driven.

### 6.13 Small samples in acquisition

October new-logo counts by channel are very small:

- Paid_search: 2 new logos
- Content: 2 new logos
- Outbound: 1 new logo
- Partner: 0 new logos

These are not enough to establish channel performance.

### 6.14 No cohort retention or LTV data

There is no cohort table showing customers by acquisition month and channel. Therefore, LTV, payback, and retention by channel cannot be calculated.

### 6.15 No resolution time or severity weighting

Support data includes severity and status, but no ticket age or resolution time. It cannot be used to calculate service-level performance or support cost drivers reliably.
