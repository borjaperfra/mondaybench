# Northstar — Monday Business Review: Data-Supported Decisions

## Decision-quality treatment used

To avoid being misled by invoice timing and data errors, I use the following definitions:

- **Booked invoice revenue**: revenue.csv as loaded, full invoice value in the invoice month.
- **MRR / ARR view**: recurring revenue normalized to a monthly/annualized view, using plans.csv and customers.csv.
- **Current active customer health**: excludes customers with `status = churned` in customers.csv, even if they were invoiced in early October before churn.
- **Usage joins**: use `usage_account_id`, not `customer_id`, because Wayne Enterprises and Prestige Worldwide have different usage IDs (`wayne_ent`, `prestige_ww`).
- **Duplicate invoice**: `INV-2026-10-0209` appears twice for Soylent, same amount and billing period, from `billing_sync`. I treat it as a probable double-load and exclude it from corrected revenue. I also show the impact if included.
- **Annual invoice**: Gringotts’ October invoice of €120,000 covers 12 months, so it adds €10,000 of MRR, not €120,000 of recurring monthly revenue.

## 3.4 CFO request: CAC payback by channel cannot be calculated

To calculate CAC payback, leadership would need at minimum:

```text
CAC = channel spend / new customers

Monthly gross margin per new customer =
    monthly revenue per customer - variable cost per customer

Payback months = CAC / monthly gross margin per new customer
```

The pack provides:

- attributed revenue by channel
- new logo vs expansion flags
- leads and paid trials
- plan-level infrastructure and support costs

But it does not provide:

- channel spend
- trial-to-paid conversion rates
- retention by channel
- customer-level gross margin
- overage pricing
- cash collection timing

Therefore, **CAC payback by acquisition channel is not computable from the supplied data**.

1. **Rate-limit behavior**
   - 13 Pro rate-limit tickets in October.
   - Rate-limit tickets increased from 5 in September to 13 in October.

2. **Capacity and usage visibility**
   - Pro utilization is 73.7% of included requests.
   - Aperture, Vandelay, and Wonka are over included volume.

3. **Overage monetization**
   - The pack says usage beyond included requests is billed separately at renewal.
   - Pro has the clearest near-term overage pressure.
   - Engineering and product should ensure overage tracking, alerts, and renewal billing are reliable.

4. **Upgrade paths**
   - Pro customers with sustained overage may need clearer upgrade paths to Enterprise or add-on capacity.

### Secondary recommendations

- **Enterprise:** run a cost-efficiency review. Enterprise contribution fell €17,300 despite nearly flat revenue.
- **Starter:** focus on onboarding, self-serve support, and pricing, not deep engineering investment.

## 5.4 For CFO: CAC payback

### Cannot be calculated from supplied data.

To calculate CAC payback by channel, leadership needs:

1. Channel spend by month.
2. New customers by channel.
3. Gross margin by plan or customer cohort.
4. Trial-to-paid conversion rates.
5. Retention by channel.
6. Expansion revenue attribution separated from new-logo revenue.

The supplied acquisition data is useful for revenue attribution, but it is not sufficient for payback.

# 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES

## 6.1 Duplicate invoice

`INV-2026-10-0209` appears twice:

- Soylent
- €19,500
- 1-month subscription
- One from `stripe`, one from `billing_sync`

If not corrected, October revenue is overstated by **€19,500**.

Recommended fix:

- Confirm whether this is a double-load.
- Exclude it from all revenue reporting.
- Add a uniqueness rule on transaction ID or invoice ID.

---

## 6.2 Annual invoice timing distortion

Gringotts’ October invoice is:

```text
€120,000 annual_subscription, billing_period_months = 12
```

If counted as full October revenue, it inflates October booked revenue.

For MRR/ARR:

```text
€120,000 / 12 = €10,000 MRR
```

The recurring revenue view should not treat the full €120,000 as October MRR.

---

## 6.3 October invoices include churned customers

October invoices were issued October 1–3. Several customers churned later in October.

Churned customers with October invoices:

- Nakatomi
- Slate Rock
- Mega Lo Mart
- Dinoco
- Buy-N-Large

These invoices should not be used to assess current customer health or current ARR.

---

## 6.4 No earned-revenue or proration data

The pack says invoices are recorded at full value in the invoice month. It does not provide:

- revenue recognition schedule
- prorated refunds
- credit notes
- churn mid-month revenue adjustments
- earned revenue by day

Therefore, we can calculate booked revenue, MRR, and ARR, but not precise earned revenue.

---

## 6.5 Expansion bookings are not current revenue

Acquisition.csv includes expansion bookings with `attributed_revenue_eur`.

The pack says expansion bookings take effect from the following billing cycle.

Therefore:

- Hooli’s September expansion is reflected in October revenue.
- Stark, Umbrella, Globex, Massive Dynamic, and Cyberdyne October expansions are not October revenue.
- They should be treated as future ARR if realized.

---

## 6.6 Funnel conversions are paid trials, not paid customers

funnel.csv defines `conversions` as leads that reached a paid trial in the same month.

It also says trial-to-paid conversion runs on a longer cycle and is not tracked.

Therefore:

- Cannot calculate lead-to-paid conversion rates.
- Cannot calculate trial-to-paid conversion rates.
- Cannot calculate CAC from leads.
- Cannot calculate CAC from trials.

---

## 6.7 No channel spend data

Acquisition and funnel data do not include spend by channel.

Therefore:

- Cannot calculate CAC.
- Cannot calculate ROAS.
- Cannot calculate payback.
- Cannot determine which channel is most efficient.

---

## 6.8 Plan costs are not fully allocated to segments

plans.csv gives plan-level infrastructure and support costs.

It does not give segment-level cost allocation.

Therefore:

- Enterprise plan includes both Enterprise-segment and Mid-market customers.
- Pro plan includes both Mid-market and SMB customers.
- Segment profitability cannot be calculated precisely without allocation rules.

---

## 6.9 Usage metrics are not a direct proxy for revenue

Acme shows why.

- Requests fell 45%.
- Tokens fell only 12%.
- Revenue was unchanged.
- Operational note explains the request drop.

Therefore, request decline alone is not sufficient to judge customer health.

---

## 6.10 Support tickets are not causal evidence

Support data shows problems, but it does not prove churn cause.

Examples:

- Nakatomi had high-severity API error tickets and churned.
- Soylent had a support spike and usage decline.
- Churned SMB Starter customers had declining usage and some billing/API tickets.

But we cannot conclude:

- support tickets caused churn
- rate limits caused churn
- billing tickets caused churn
- product issues caused contraction

without resolution time, CSAT, customer interviews, or churn reason data.

---

## 6.11 No overage pricing

plans.csv says usage beyond included requests is billed separately at renewal.

But the pack does not provide:

- overage price per request
- overage price per token
- current overage billing history
- renewal terms

Therefore, we can identify overage pressure on Pro, but we cannot quantify future overage revenue.
