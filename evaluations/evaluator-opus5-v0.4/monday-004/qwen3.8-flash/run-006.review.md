### 5.4 CFO: CAC payback by acquisition channel

**Cannot be calculated reliably from the supplied data.**

To calculate CAC payback by channel, we need:

1. **Channel spend**
   - Not provided.

2. **New paying customers by channel**
   - acquisition.csv only includes converted leads, but it mixes new logos and expansions.
   - funnel.csv tracks paid trials, not paid conversions.
   - trial-to-paid conversion is not tracked.

3. **Gross margin by customer or plan**
   - We have plan-level contribution, but not channel-level plan mix or customer-level costs.
   - Segment costs cannot be reliably derived from plan costs.

4. **Revenue timing**
   - Expansion bookings take effect the following billing cycle.
   - October attributed expansion revenue is not October revenue.

Formula that cannot be completed:

```text
CAC by channel = channel spend / new paying customers by channel

Payback months = CAC / monthly gross margin per new customer
```

**What can be provided now:**

- attributed ARR by channel;
- new-logo vs expansion split;
- paid-trial volume and rate by channel;
- timing mismatch between conversion and revenue.

**What cannot be provided now:**

- CAC;
- CAC payback;
- channel ROI;
- true lead-to-paid conversion rate;
- causal channel performance.

   - Nakatomi, Slate Rock, Mega Lo Mart, Dinoco, and Buy-N-Large churned during October but were invoiced on October 1.
   - This inflates October recurring revenue if used as a run-rate.

4. **No proration guidance**
   - The data say invoices are recorded at full value in the month dated.
   - We do not have a recognised-revenue proration policy.
   - True recognised October revenue may be lower than invoice-based revenue.

5. **Professional services are non-recurring**
   - September had €51k of professional services.
   - October had €5.7k.
   - This decline is masked by the annual invoice and duplicate.

---

### 6.2 Customer and ARR data issues

1. **customers.csv is current as of this morning**
   - It is not a historical snapshot.
   - It includes current ARR, not necessarily September ARR.
   - For historical revenue, invoices are better.
   - For current run-rate, customers.csv is better.

2. **Pending expansions are not in current run-rate**
   - October expansion bookings total €216k ARR.
   - They take effect from the following billing cycle.
   - They should not be treated as October revenue.

3. **Churned customers still have ARR values**
   - Churned customers have `arr_eur` populated.
   - They must be excluded from active ARR.

---

### 6.3 Usage data issues

1. **Request volume alone is misleading**
   - Acme requests fell 45%, but tokens fell only 12%.
   - The operational note explains a shift to batch runs.
   - Do not infer Acme health decline from requests alone.

2. **usage_change_pct is only for requests**
   - It does not capture tokens or active users.
   - It is blank for new customers with no prior month.

3. **No proof of causality for Acme**
   - The request drop is consistent with the expected batch migration, but the data do not prove causality.

---

### 6.4 Acquisition data issues

1. **No channel spend**
   - CAC cannot be calculated.
   - CAC payback cannot be calculated.

2. **acquisition.csv only includes converted leads**
   - It does not include non-converted leads.
   - It cannot produce full funnel conversion rates.

3. **funnel.csv conversions are paid trials, not paid customers**
   - The file says conversions are leads that reached a paid trial in the same month.
   - Trial-to-paid conversion is not tracked.

4. **Expansion attribution is not necessarily causal**
   - Paid_search is credited with large expansion ARR.
   - We cannot conclude paid_search caused those expansions.

5. **Expansion timing mismatch**
   - October expansion bookings take effect next billing cycle.
   - October attributed revenue is not October recognised revenue.

6. **Small sample sizes**
   - Only 14 converted leads total across two months.
   - Channel conclusions are fragile.

---

### 6.5 Plan and segment economics issues

1. **Plan costs are not segment costs**
   - Enterprise plan includes both Enterprise-segment and Mid-market-segment customers.
   - Pro plan includes Mid-market and SMB customers.
   - We cannot reliably calculate segment profitability.

2. **Support cost includes CS staff by plan**
   - It is not allocated by segment.
   - It may not reflect actual customer-level support cost.

3. **October plan customer counts include churned customers**
   - Pro October customer count includes Nakatomi.
   - Starter October customer count includes four churned customers.
   - Per-customer economics may be distorted.

4. **Overage billing occurs at renewal**
   - Customers above included requests are not necessarily generating overage revenue yet.
   - We cannot calculate future overage revenue reliably.

---

### 6.6 Support data issues

1. **No resolution time**
   - We can count tickets, but not time-to-resolution.

2. **No reopen status**
   - We cannot identify recurring issues.

3. **No CSAT or NPS**
   - We cannot measure satisfaction directly.

4. **No support cost per ticket**
   - Support cost is only available by plan.

5. **Churned customers still raise tickets**
   - October support tickets include customers who churned during the month.
