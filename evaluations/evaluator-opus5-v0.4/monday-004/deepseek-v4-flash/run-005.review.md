## Full response

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Headline revenue is misleading.** October invoiced revenue is **€873.4k** vs September **€797.1k** (+9.6%), but this is inflated by Gringotts’ **€120k annual invoice** and €5.7k professional services. Underlying MRR only grew from **€746.1k** to **€757.7k** (+1.6%).
- **On an active-customer basis, MRR actually fell.** Churned customers were still invoiced on 1 October. Adjusting for churn, active MRR is roughly **€732.4k**, down **~1.8%** from September.
- **Acme’s request drop is expected, not a health problem.** Requests fell 45%, but tokens fell only 12% and active users fell only 3%, consistent with the move to scheduled batch workloads.
- **SMB / Starter is the weakest area.** SMB had 4 churns in October, the highest support load relative to revenue, and Starter has the lowest contribution per customer.
- **Content is the best-supported channel to increase spend** for new logo acquisition. Paid search is generating more attributed revenue, but mostly from expansions of existing Enterprise accounts, not new customers.
- **CAC payback cannot be calculated** from this data pack because no channel spend or cost data is provided.

So the real story is:

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Reduce investment in the SMB segment, specifically the Starter plan.**  
   The data supports this: 4 of 13 SMB customers churned in October, SMB has the highest support tickets per €100k MRR, and Starter has the lowest contribution per customer. Do not cut all SMB activity — it is producing new logos — but reduce spend on Starter acquisition until unit economics and retention improve.

2. **Increase spend on the content channel.**  
   Content has the strongest new-logo performance, reasonable trial conversion, and is not dependent on expansion bookings from existing Enterprise accounts. Paid search should be treated as an expansion channel, not the primary new-customer channel. Before scaling, finance should provide channel cost data so CAC payback can actually be calculated.

3. **Give the Starter plan the next quarter’s engineering focus.**  
   It is the weakest plan after cost: lowest contribution per customer, high churn, and disproportionate support load. A secondary focus should be Enterprise cost efficiency, because Enterprise is 87% of MRR and its margin is eroding.

## 6. Uncertainties / Data Issues

- **Duplicate invoice:** `INV-2026-10-0209` for Soylent appears twice, once from `stripe` and once from `billing_sync`. October revenue should be deduplicated.
- **Gringotts annual invoice:** The €120k October invoice inflates reported revenue. Use MRR for trend analysis.
- **Churned customers still in October data:** Nakatomi and four Starter customers were invoiced on 1 October and appear in revenue, plans, and usage. Active-base adjustments are necessary.
- **Funnel conversions are trial starts, not paid conversions.** Trial-to-paid conversion is not tracked, so end-to-end funnel conversion cannot be calculated.
- **No acquisition cost data:** CAC payback by channel cannot be calculated reliably.
- **Attributed revenue is annualised at conversion, not cash received.** Expansion bookings take effect the following billing cycle.
- **Soylent’s September invoice (€22k) does not match its current ARR (€234k → €19.5k/month).** This suggests a contract change that is not explicitly recorded.
- **Usage account IDs differ from customer IDs for some accounts** (e.g., `wayne_ent`, `prestige_ww`), so joins must use `usage_account_id`.
