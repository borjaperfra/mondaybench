# Northstar Business Review — Monday Analysis

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
- **Revenue grew modestly (~2.9% MoM on normalized MRR)**, driven by stable Enterprise renewals and Gringotts’ new annual contract. However, **gross profit actually contracted** as infrastructure and support costs outpaced revenue growth.
- **SMB segment is bleeding customers**: 4 of 9 active SMB customers churned in October (44% monthly churn rate). Pre-churn usage dropped ~35%, signaling early disengagement.
- **Partner channel is the most efficient acquisition lever**: 22–25% trial-to-paid conversion and highest average ARR per new logo (~€83k), though pipeline volume is low.
- **Starter plan has the lowest margin (32%) and highest churn**, while **Enterprise plan shows margin compression (29.5%)** due to rising infra/support costs. Pro plan remains the healthiest (~63% margin) but is small.
- **CAC payback cannot be calculated** from the provided data pack due to missing marketing spend figures.

- October subscription revenue: €757,700
- Gringotts annual contract (€120k) inflates October cash collection. Normalized to MRR (€10k/mo), October MRR = €767,700.
- **MoM growth: +2.9%**. Growth is flat when excluding the one-time annual contract.

**Segment Health**

## 5. RECOMMENDATIONS
1. **Customer Segment**: **Reduce/redirect investment in SMB**. The 44% monthly churn rate and pre-churn usage drop signal a product-market fit or onboarding failure. Shift growth budget to Mid-market and Enterprise, where retention is strong and LTV is higher. Implement a mandatory SMB retention program (success check-ins, usage alerts, tiered pricing review).
2. **Acquisition Channel**: **Increase spend on Partner channel**. It delivers 3x the conversion rate and 2.5x the ARR per new logo vs. Paid Search. Partner spend is capped by volume, so work with partners to expand co-marketing and referral pipelines. Simultaneously, optimize Paid Search landing pages to reverse the conversion rate decline (8.5% → 6.4%).
3. **Product Plan Focus**: **Prioritize Starter retention and infra cost optimization for Enterprise**. 
   - *Starter*: Fix onboarding and early-value delivery to stop the churn bleed. Consider bundling support or introducing usage-based overage pricing to improve unit economics.
   - *Enterprise*: Audit infrastructure scaling logic. Costs rose €8.8k without revenue growth. Implement request caching, batch processing incentives, or tiered infra pricing to protect margin.
4. **CFO Request (CAC Payback)**: **Cannot be calculated yet**. Marketing spend data is missing from the pack. Finance must provide channel-level ad spend, agency fees, and tool costs to compute CAC and payback periods. Until then, allocate budget based on conversion rate and ARR quality (Partner > Paid Search > Content > Outbound).

## 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES
- **Missing CAC Spend Data**: The acquisition and funnel files contain lead/conversion counts and attributed revenue, but zero marketing spend figures. CAC and payback periods cannot be calculated.
- **Duplicate Invoice**: Soylent has two identical €19,500 subscription invoices in October (one from `stripe`, one from `billing_sync`). This inflates October revenue by €19.5k and should be reconciled.
- **Funnel vs. Actual Conversions**: Funnel conversions (100 in Sept, 120 in Oct) vastly exceed actual paid conversions (5 in Sept, 9 in Oct). The pack notes trial-to-paid runs on a longer cycle, but the lag is untracked, making channel efficiency metrics forward-looking only.
- **Gringotts Annual Contract**: The €120k October invoice is a 12-month contract. Treating it as monthly revenue overstates Oct cash flow. Normalization to €10k MRR is required for accurate trend analysis.
- **Causal Claims Unsupported**: Acme’s 45% usage drop aligns with the operational note, but SMB usage drops correlate with churn, not a known product change. We cannot attribute usage declines to specific product features without telemetry.
- **Support Cost Allocation**: `support_cost_eur` in `plans.csv` is fully loaded but not broken down by ticket type or resolution time. High-severity open tickets in Enterprise may indicate capacity constraints rather than product defects, but the data doesn’t distinguish.
