Leadership should **reduce investment** here and reallocate resources to higher-margin segments.
- **Acquisition Channel:** The **Partner channel** is the most efficient driver of growth. It delivers a 23.7% trial-to-paid conversion rate, €5.2k attributed revenue per conversion, and exclusively brings in high-value new logos. **Increase spend** on Partner-led acquisition.

1. **Reduce Investment in SMB (Starter Segment):** Halt active growth spend here. Shift to a fully automated, self-serve onboarding flow. Consider raising minimum contract terms or bundling Starter with limited support to protect margins. Reallocate budget to Pro and Enterprise.
2. **Increase Spend on Partner Channel:** Double down on Partner-led acquisition. The 23.7% conversion rate and €5.2k/conv yield indicate strong product-market fit and low friction. Negotiate volume-based partner incentives to scale this channel while maintaining margin.
3. **Direct Engineering Focus to the Pro Plan:** Invest in features that increase stickiness and expand usage within the Pro tier (e.g., advanced rate-limiting controls, team collaboration, usage analytics). The 62.7% margin and low support load make it the highest-ROI target for product development.
4. **Enterprise Support Triage:** While not cutting investment, assign a dedicated platform engineering pod to address recurring `api_errors` and `performance` tickets. Proactive monitoring and SDK improvements will protect the 100% retention rate and stabilize the 29.5% margin.
5. **CAC Payback Analysis:** Finance must supply channel spend data to calculate true CAC and payback periods. Until then, use Partner channel as the benchmark for efficient acquisition.

# 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES

- **Missing Marketing Spend:** The acquisition pack lacks channel cost data. CAC and payback periods cannot be calculated. Any payback recommendation is currently speculative.
- **Invoice vs. Churn Timing:** Churned customers (nakatomi, slate-rock, mega-lo-mart, dinoco, buy-n-large) still show October subscription invoices. These are likely final/prorated bills or data lag. Revenue recognition may be overstated for October if these are not credited back.
- **Customer Count Mismatch:** `plans.csv` reports 13 Starter customers in October, but the raw `customers.csv` shows 14 active SMBs. One account may be in a grace period or data sync delay. This slightly skews per-customer averages.
- **Usage Attribution Lag:** `usage_change_pct` is missing for several new October accounts (spacely, cogswell, bishop, gekko, paper-street). Without September baselines, we cannot assess early adoption velocity for these new logos.
- **Expansion Booking Recognition:** The pack notes expansion bookings take effect from the next billing cycle, yet `acquisition.csv` credits `attributed_revenue_eur` at conversion. This creates a timing mismatch between marketing attribution and actual revenue recognition, which could distort channel ROI if not adjusted.
- **Support Cost Allocation:** `support_cost_eur` in `plans.csv` is allocated by plan, not segment. While Pro/Enterprise/Starter map cleanly to Mid-market/Enterprise/SMB, any cross-plan support sharing is hidden, potentially understating SMB support burden.
