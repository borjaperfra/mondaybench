- **The headline revenue growth is an illusion.** Invoiced revenue rose +9.6% (€797.1k → €873.4k), but underlying MRR grew only **+1.6%** (€746.1k → €757.7k). The gap comes from Gringotts' €120k annual invoice (12 months booked in one), a **duplicate soylent invoice (€19.5k)**, and advance billing that still includes €25.3k MRR from five customers who churned during October.

All five showed usage collapses before churning (-16% to -37%) — a usable early-warning signal. **Soylent (€234k ARR) is showing the same signature right now** (-27% usage, 8 tickets including the pack's only critical, invoice already cut -11.4%).

## 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES

1. **Duplicate invoice:** INV-2026-10-0209 (soylent, €19,500) appears twice (stripe 1 Oct; billing_sync 2 Oct, same ID/amount). Naive sums overstate October by €19,500. Investigate the billing_sync pipeline.

3. **Attribution quality:** €252k of the €631.2k credited ARR is expansion of existing customers (83% to paid_search); only hooli's is verified in billing; €216k is unverified until November invoices.
4. **Trial-to-paid not tracked**; funnel "conversions" are trials. September trials may still convert (right-censoring), biasing recent-month comparisons.
5. **Advance-billing distortions:** October contains €25.3k MRR from mid-month churners and €110k of Gringotts' annual invoice covering future months; plans.csv October counts also include churned customers (nakatomi + 4 starters).
6. **Only two months of history:** September churn = 0, so the October spike has no baseline; conclusions rest on tiny n (5 churns, 9 new logos, 15 acquisition rows). Directional, not statistical.
7. **Overage revenue can't be sized:** arr_eur may or may not include overage; Acme's fall below its included threshold is a renewal risk we cannot quantify, as are stark/wayne true-ups.
8. **Soylent's invoice cut** (22,000 → 19,500) has no recorded cause (downgrade vs credits vs correction) — don't assume.
9. **Join-key fragility:** usage_account_id differs from customer_id for two accounts (wayne_ent, prestige_ww) — joins must use the customers.usage_account_id bridge; support.csv uses customer_id. Churned rows retain arr_eur — summing without filtering status overstates active ARR by €303,600 (€9,092,400 vs €8,788,800).

11. **Acme:** -45% is consistent with the stated 40–50% expected reduction, but the data doesn't separate interactive vs batch traffic, so residual demand loss can't be ruled out.
