## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1. **Headline October revenue is overstated.** Naively summed, October invoices show ~+12% MoM growth. After removing a **duplicate invoice** (INV-2026-10-0209, soylent €19,500, loaded from both `stripe` and `billing_sync`) and treating gringotts' **€120,000 annual prepayment** at its €10,000/month equivalent, underlying subscription revenue grew only **+1.6% MoM** (€746.1k → €757.7k).
2. **Profit went down while revenue went up.** Total plan profit fell **€262.9k → €246.5k (−€16.4k)** as costs grew +5.8% against +1.6% revenue. The entire decline comes from the **enterprise plan** (margin 32.2% → 29.5%).
3. **Churn is real but concentrated:** 5 logos / **€303.6k ARR** lost in October (3.4% of opening ARR in one month) — 4 low-ARR SMB starter accounts plus nakatomi (€180k, mid-market). SMB is simultaneously the biggest churn source **and** the only logo engine (8 of 9 new logos; net SMB ARR **+€123.6k** over two months). **A wholesale segment cut is not supported.**
4. **The paid_search "breakout" is mostly attribution inflation.** 76% of paid_search's attributed revenue (€210k of €274.8k) is *expansion* credit from 2023/24-vintage enterprise accounts, booked against a campaign that started 2026-09-28. **No channel spend exists in this pack → CAC and payback cannot be calculated.** Do not raise spend yet.
5. **Product risk signal:** chronic high-severity `api_errors` on the enterprise plan. 3 of 5 churned accounts had high-sev API issues near churn. **~€3.23M ARR** (stark, wayne, contoso, soylent) currently has open high-sev tickets; wayne renews in ~5 weeks.
6. **Acme's −45% request drop is not a problem** — it is the documented batch migration (40–50% reduction was the plan estimate; tokens fell only −12%). Do not treat it as a churn signal.

**Verdicts on the three decisions:** (1) No segment cut this week — targeted retention instead; (2) No paid_search increase yet — get spend data first; (3) Enterprise plan reliability gets the quarter's engineering focus, with the pro upgrade path as the highest-ROI secondary.
