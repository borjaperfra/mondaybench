To: Northstar Leadership Team

Subject: Business Review: September–October 2026 Performance

---

### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The business is experiencing a divergence between high-value stability and low-end volatility. While **Enterprise revenue remains the bedrock** of the company, we are seeing significant usage shifts in key accounts (Acme Corp) and a churn trend in the SMB segment. 

*   **Key Finding:** The "Enterprise" segment is highly stable but faces a technical risk (API errors/performance) that correlates with high-value usage.
*   **Growth Opportunity:** "Paid Search" is the most efficient engine for new logos, though "Content" shows higher conversion quality.
*   **Product Focus:** The **Enterprise plan** requires immediate engineering attention to address stability (API/Performance) to protect the core revenue, despite the "Pro" plan showing higher relative margin.

---

### 2. REVENUE & CUSTOMER HEALTH
Headline revenue is heavily skewed by the Enterprise segment.

*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   **Enterprise:** Drives ~90% of total ARR. It is stable, but usage patterns are shifting. Notably, **Acme Corp** saw a 45% drop in request volume in October due to a shift to batch processing.
    *   **Mid-Market:** Showing moderate growth, but the loss of **Nakatomi** (churned Oct) represents a loss of €180k ARR.
    *   **SMB:** High churn risk. Four SMB customers churned in October (Slate-rock, Mega-lo-mart, Dinoco, Buy-n-large).
*   **Revenue Drivers:** October revenue was bolstered by a large annual upfront payment from **Gringotts** (€120k), which masks the underlying monthly subscription trends.

---

### 3. ACQUISITION
We can evaluate channel efficiency using the Funnel and Acquisition data.

| Channel | Sept Leads $\rightarrow$ Conv % | Oct Leads $\rightarrow$ Conv % | Oct Conversions | Avg. Attributed ARR |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paid Search** | 8.6% | 6.4% | 58 | €45,000* |
| **Content** | 11.9% | 10.3% | 34 | €23,000* |
| **Partner** | 25.0% | 22.6% | 17 | €132,000* |
| **Outbound** | 6.2% | 5.6% | 11 | €22,800* |

*\*Note: Paid Search includes high-value expansion bookings (e.g., Stark Industries, Umbrella Health), making its "per lead" value significantly higher than others.*

*   **Decision Support:** **Increase spend on Paid Search.** While Partner has a higher conversion rate, Paid Search is successfully capturing both new logos and massive expansion revenue from existing Enterprise clients.

---

### 4. PLAN ECONOMICS
We must look at **Gross Margin per Plan** (Revenue minus Infra and Support costs).

| Plan | Oct Revenue | Oct Total Cost | Oct Gross Margin | Margin % |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enterprise** | €659,500 | €464,800 | €194,700 | **29.5%** |
| **Pro** | €66,200 | €24,700 | €41,500 | **62.7%** |
| **Starter** | €32,000 | €21,700 | €10,300 | **32.2%** |

*   **Analysis:** The **Pro plan** is the most economically efficient. However, the **Enterprise plan** is the most "expensive" to run due to massive infrastructure requirements (nearly 45% of revenue).
*   **Decision Support:** Engineering focus should be on the **Enterprise plan**. While Pro has better margins, the Enterprise plan's stability is critical to the company's survival.

---

### 5. RECOMMENDATIONS
1.  **Do NOT reduce investment in any customer segment yet.** While SMB churn is high, the Enterprise segment is the only one providing the scale required to cover fixed costs. Instead, pivot SMB strategy toward "Starter" automation to reduce support overhead.
2.  **Increase spend on Paid Search.** It is the primary driver for both new logos and high-value expansion.
3.  **Direct Engineering focus to the Enterprise Plan.** Support data shows a high concentration of "High" severity tickets (API errors and Performance) among Enterprise clients (Acme, Stark, Wayne, Umbrella). Protecting this €659k/month revenue is the priority.

---

### 6. UNCERTAINTIES / DATA ISSUES
*   **CAC Payback:** The CFO's request for CAC payback cannot be fully fulfilled because **marketing spend (cost) per channel is not provided** in the dataset. We can only calculate "Revenue per Lead."
*   **Causal Claims:** We cannot definitively say if the drop in Acme Corp's usage caused a revenue drop, as the revenue file shows they are still paying their monthly subscription.
*   **Data Discrepancy:** The `usage_change_pct` for Acme Corp (-45%) is a known operational shift (batching), not necessarily a loss of customer interest, which could skew "health" metrics if not interpreted correctly.
*   **Expansion Attribution:** Expansion revenue is credited to channels in the `acquisition.csv`, but the timing of when these "expansion" leads actually convert is not granularly tracked, making long-term ROI calculations difficult.
